## Systems outside the book

The defensive chapters cover the systems Wbridge5 itself plays: its own,
SEF and SAYC.  A strong 1♣, Multi 2♦, Flannery 2♦, Precision 2♦ and Roman
2♦ belong to none of them and are out of scope.

Wbridge5 has no setting for these openings and gets no alerts, so it reads
them by its own system.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that it defends
(1♣)-P-(1♦) as two natural bids, cuebidding 2♣ with 5-5 in the majors (246
of 300 hands) and overcalling a natural 2♦ with 6+♦ (162 of 300).  It takes
any (2♦) for a strong, game-forcing 2♦: with 8--18 HCP it passes 201 of 300
hands and never makes a takeout double, and a long diamond suit bids a
natural 3♦, as with ♠J2 ♥54 ♦AK109854 ♣Q10.
