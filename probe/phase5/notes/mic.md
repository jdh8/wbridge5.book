## Reading of the doc section

- Parenthesised calls are OURS (the doc's defensive notation, cf. line 1037
  `1X-(?)`).  "敵方溝通一門牌組" = opponents have shown one suit (opened);
  "敵方溝通兩門牌組" = opponents have shown two suits (1X - 1Y).  So parts
  四--六 are NOT the opponents' conventions: they are advances to OUR
  two-suited calls after (1X) P (1Y): our Dbl, our passed-hand Sandwich 1NT,
  our cuebid.  The prompt's reading (opponents use them) is covered instead
  by part D below (opponents' Michaels/UNT over our opening), which the
  prompt also asked for.
- Line 1408: preference order majors first; between two majors or two
  minors, the cheaper one.
- chm Michael.htm: "Le répondant doit obligatoirement annoncer la meilleure
  des deux couleurs" (advancer must name the better of the two suits).  The
  option replaces the strong (20+) cuebid.  Also: (1♣) 3♣ = ♦+♠ 55 and
  (1♦) 3♦ = ♣+♠ 55 (already in 1y.md as jump cuebids).
- chm conventions.htm SANDWICH: "Après un passe d'entrée, 1SA ... 5 cartes
  dans la couleur la moins chère non nommée et 4 cartes dans l'autre".
  Doc line 1454: 4 major + 5 minor.
