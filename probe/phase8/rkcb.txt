# and what 4NT asks after a control bid by each partner.
# Notes: probe/phase8/notes/rkcb.md.  Tabulate answers with

# (keys = aces + trump K; trump K vs aces tells RKCB 0314 from Blackwood).

# ===== Part 1: South chooses between 4NT and a control bid =====
# Existing data already settles most of it (see notes): South cues the
# cheapest controlled suit whenever one exists below 4NT, whatever its keys
# (up to 5) or strength (to 25 HCP), and bids 4NT only with no control to cue.
# Gaps below: very strong hands with 3-4 aces in a cue auction; a direct 4NT
# by responder over 1M; a strong opener facing a minor raise.
1S-P-2H-P-3H-P --H 5-6 --S 0-2 --hcp 17-24 --aces 3-4 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/1SP2HP3HP~a34.jsonl
1S-P --S 4-5 --hcp 17-24 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/1SP~S45hcp1724.jsonl
1D-P-3D-P --D 5-6 --S 0-3 --H 0-3 --C 0-4 --hcp 16-21 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/1DP3DP~hcp1621.jsonl
# Fixed hands with no control to cue (smoke: all four bid 4NT, never a cue).
# (A 2C hand with only a diamond control could not be built: 23+ HCP with no
# spade or club A, K or shortness needs both red aces, which opens 2D.)
1S-P-2H-P-3H-P --hand QJ.AKQJ32.Q32.J2 -n 1 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/hand-1SP2HP3HP-QJ.jsonl
1S-P-2H-P-3H-P --hand Q2.AKQJ32.QJ2.QJ -n 1 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/hand-1SP2HP3HP-Q2.jsonl
1D-P-1H-P-3H-P --hand QJ2.AKQ2.QJ2.J32 -n 1 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/hand-1DP1HP3HP-QJ2.jsonl
1D-P-1H-P-3H-P --hand QJ2.AKQJ.QJ2.J32 -n 1 --seed 1 -o probe/data/p8/rkcb/hand-1DP1HP3HP-AKQJ.jsonl
