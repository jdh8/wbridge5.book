## Hypotheses

1. 16+ HCP: no jump; Wbridge5 makes the same positive as at 10--15 (2M with
   5+M, 2NT balanced, 3m with a good 6m), and shows strength later.  Smoke
   (20 random 16--22): 2NT 12, 2♠ 3, 2♥ 2, 3♦ 2, and 7♥ once with 21 HCP
   and AKQ8432 of hearts.  With 5+♠ at 16--22: 2♠ 19, 2NT 1.  So mostly
   confirmed; direct grand-slam jumps exist at 21+.
2. 2♣ - 2♠ - 3♠: responder never signs off in 4♠ (2♣ is 19+ and responder
   10+): control bids up the line or 4NT RKCB.  Smoke (13): 4♣ 4, 4♦ 4,
   4NT 3, 4♥ 2.  Check whether 4♠ ever appears, and 4NT vs CTRL by HCP.
3. 2♣ - 2♠ - 2NT: as 2♣ - 2♥ - 2NT: 3♠ 6+♠, new suit 4+, 3♥ 4♥, 3NT/4NT
   quantitative with 5-3-3-2.  Smoke (13): 3♠ 3 (6♠), 4NT 3 (5332, 10--11
   HCP), 3♣ 3, 3♦ 3, 3♥ 1 (4♥).  No 3NT seen yet: 4NT at 10--11 suggests the
   2♣ - 2♥ - 2NT split (3NT 9--10, 4NT 10--11) moves or vanishes.
4. 2♣ - 2♠ - 3♥/3♦: raise with 3+ (or 4+?), 3♠ with 6♠, 3NT otherwise.
5. 2♣ - 2NT - 3♠/3♥: raise with 3+, 3NT with 2, cue/new suit?
6. 2♣ - 2NT - 3NT: pass at the bottom, 4NT quantitative, 4m natural with a
   5-card minor.  Smoke (19): P 6 (10--14), 4NT 5 (10--14), 4♦ 4 (5♦),
   4♣ 4 (5♣).  The P/4NT split is not by HCP alone; look at controls.
7. 2♣ - 2NT - 3♣/3♦: raise with 4+, 3M with 4 cards?, 3NT.
8. 2♣ - 3♣ - 3NT: smoke (13): 4NT 9, 4♣ 2, 4♥ 1, P 1.  Is 4NT RKCB for
   clubs or quantitative?  The chapter already says 4NT after 2♣ - 2♦ - 3♥
   is "presumably RKCB"; opener's answer cannot be probed (North is
   scripted).  Report as a slam try, Ace-count check with keys.py if the
   meaning matters.
9. 2♣ - 3♣ - 4♣: control bids.  Smoke (13): 4♦ 9, 4♥ 2, 4♠ 1, 4NT 1.
10. 2♣ - 3♣♦ - 5m: responder raises to 6.  Smoke 3♦ - 5♦ (14): 6♦ 13, P 1
    (9 HCP).  So 5m is invitational, not a signoff.
11. 2♣ - 3♣♦ - 3M: raise with 3+, 3NT, rebid the minor with 7.
