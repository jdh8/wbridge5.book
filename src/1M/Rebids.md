# Constructive rebids

This chapter covers the uncontested auctions after 1♥♠ outside the
[semiforcing 1NT](1NT.md) and [forcing 2/1](TwoOverOne.md): 1♥ - 1♠, the
raises, and the strong responses.  The responses themselves are in [the parent
chapter](../1M.md).

The ranges come from probing Wbridge5 5.12 on about 250 boards per auction.
Ranges are in points, i.e. HCP plus distribution and length points, except
where marked HCP.

## After 1♥ - 1♠

Opener raises spades only with 4 cards.  With 3 spades and a minimum, opener
rebids 1NT.  There is no reverse because 2♠ is the raise, so opener shows
extra strength by jumping.

| 1♥ - 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT  | 11--14 HCP, 2--3♠, 5=♥, BAL
| 2♣♦  | 13--18, 4+#
| 2♥   | 13--17, 6+♥
| 2♠   | 13--16, 4=♠
| 2NT  | 18--21 HCP, BAL
| 3♣♦  | 18--21, 4+#
| 3♥   | 17--21, 6+♥
| 3♠   | 16--20, 4=♠
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 19--22, **0--1#**, 4=♠
| 4♥   | 19--22, 7+♥
| 4♠   | 19--21, 4=♠, 5-4-2-2

Wbridge5 rebids 2NT with any balanced 18--21 HCP and never rebids 3NT here.
For example, ♠K54 ♥AK643 ♦AK3 ♣Q9 rebids 2NT.  With ♠A1042 ♥AKJ75 ♦7 ♣A97,
opener jumps to 3♠ rather than splintering, but ♠A976 ♥AKJ952 ♦A96 ♣- bids a
4♣ splinter.

### Responder's rebids after 1NT

Responder's 2♣ is [Roudy](../Glossary.md#roudy), which asks about spade
support and strength.  It needs 5+ spades.  With 4 spades, responder bids
notrump directly.

| 1♥ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 6--10 HCP
| 2♣! | Roudy, INV+, 10+ HCP, 5+♠
| 2♦  | NF, 8--10 HCP, 5=♠, 4=♦
| 2♥  | Preference, 6--11 HCP, 2=♥
| 2♠  | S/O, 6--10 HCP, 6+♠
| 2NT | INV, 11--12 HCP
| 3♠  | INV, 7--9 HCP, 7+♠
| 3NT | S/O, 12--16 HCP, 4=♠
| 4♠  | S/O, 7+♠

For example, ♠AKQ1093 ♥J10 ♦Q53 ♣104 bids 2♣.  Opener answers in the
short-long-long-short code.  A minimum has 11--13 HCP and a maximum has
13--14 HCP.

| 1♥ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - 2♣! - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | Minimum, 2=♠
| 2♥!  | Minimum, 3=♠
| 2♠!  | Maximum, 3=♠
| 2NT! | Maximum, 2=♠

### Responder's rebids after a new suit

The fourth suit 2♦ is forcing.

| 1♥ - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 6--10 HCP, 3+♣
| 2♦! | FSF, 10+ HCP
| 2♥  | Preference, 6--9 HCP, 2=♥
| 2♠  | S/O, 6--10 HCP, 6+♠
| 2NT | INV, 10--13 HCP
| 3♣  | INV, 8--11 HCP, 4=♣
| 3♠  | INV, 13--16, 6+♠
| 3NT | S/O, 13--16 HCP
| 4♣  | S/T, 14+ HCP, 5♠ 4♣
| 4♠  | S/O, 6+♠

The 4♣ row comes from 4 boards.  The 2011 writeup says that only a fourth suit
at the 2-level in a minor is forcing, so 1♥ - 1♠ - 2♦ - 3♣ would be natural.
That auction was not probed.

## Simple raise

Opener's game tries are natural.  A new suit shows 4+ cards and invites game,
and a jump to the 4-level is a splinter.

| 1♥ - 2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | 13--16
| 2♠   | Game try, 16--19, 4=♠
| 3♣♦  | Game try, 16--19, 4+#
| 3♥   | INV, 17--18, 6+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, 20+, **0--1♠**
| 3NT  | 19--21 HCP, 5-3-3-2
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 20+, **0--1#**
| 4♥   | S/O, 18+

The 3♠ row comes from 3 boards.  Opener also bid 2NT on 3 boards with
15 points and 5-3-3-2.

| 1♠ - 2♠ - | |
|-------|-|
| P     | 13--16
| 3♣♦♥  | Game try, 16--19, 4+#
| 3♠    | INV, 17--18, 6+♠
| 3NT   | 18--19 HCP, 5-3-3-2
| 4♣♦♥! | SPL, 20+, **0--1#**
| 4♠    | S/O, 18+

For example, ♠A72 ♥AQJ98 ♦K762 ♣8 makes a 3♦ game try over 1♥ - 2♥.  With
♠K82 ♥AKJ63 ♦AK82 ♣6, opener bids 4♥.

Responder accepts a game try with 9+ HCP, or with 7--8 HCP and 5+ cards in
the asking suit.  After 1♥ - 2♥ - 3♣, ♠A42 ♥K52 ♦8654 ♣Q43 bids 4♥.  The
2011 writeup's rule, 8+ points or a double fit, fits the data.

## Bergen raises

Opener signs off or bids game.  Wbridge5 never control-bids over the mixed
raise 3♣, even with 20+ points.

| 1M - 3♣! - | |
|-----|-|
| 3M  | S/O, 13--15
| 3NT | 18--19 HCP, 5-3-3-2
| 4M  | S/O, 16+

Over the limit raise, the game threshold drops to 14 points.  Only 1♥ - 3♦
was probed.

| 1♥ - 3♦! - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♥  | S/O, 13--14
| 3♠! | S/T, 17+, ♠ control
| 3NT | 18--19 HCP, 5-3-3-2
| 4♥  | S/O, 14+

The 2011 writeup has opener control-bid with 20--21 over 3♣ and with 19--21
over 3♦.  Over 3♦, Wbridge5 makes a 3♠ slam try with 17+ points.

After 1M - 3♣ - 3M, responder passes.  The 2011 writeup says that responder
bids game with 5 trumps, but Wbridge5 passed on 92 of 97 boards with 5 hearts
and 7--9 HCP.

## Special 2NT

### Unpassed responder

Opener relays with almost every hand, but splinters with 17+ points and
shortness.  The relay continuations are in [the parent
chapter](../1M.md#puppet-special-2nt).

| 1M - 2NT! - | |
|----------------|-|
| 3♣!            | Relay, any hand without a splinter
| Jump new suit! | SPL, 17+, **0--1#**

Over 1♥, the splinters are 3♠, 4♣, and 4♦.  Over 1♠, they are 4♣, 4♦, and
4♥.  For example, ♠K82 ♥AKJ63 ♦AK82 ♣6 bids 4♣ over 1♥ - 2NT.  The 2011
writeup has these jumps as control bids, but every probed splinter held a
singleton or void in the bid suit.

### Passed responder

A passed responder's 2NT is a limit raise.  Opener signs off at the 3- or
4-level, or tries for slam.

| P - 1♥ -<br>2NT! - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣♦ | S/T, 18+, 4+#
| 3♥  | S/O, 12--14
| 3♠! | S/T, 20+, ♠ control
| 3NT | 14--19 HCP, 5-3-3-2
| 4♥  | S/O, 15+

The 3♠ row comes from 7 boards, and every hand held the ♠A or ♠K.  The 3NT
row comes from 4 boards.

## Fit-showing jumps

Opener may pass a fit-showing jump with a minimum and a fit for responder's
suit.  Over 2♠, opener can also choose spades as trumps.

| P - 1♥ -<br>2♠! - | |
|------|-|
| P    | 12--13, 3♠
| 3♣♦  | S/T, 18+, 4+#
| 3♥   | S/O, 12--14
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 16+, **0--1#**
| 4♥   | 15+
| 4♠   | 13+, 3--4♠

For example, ♠J53 ♥AJ532 ♦K94 ♣K10 passes 2♠.

| P - 1♠ -<br>3♣! - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♦♥ | S/T, 17+, 4+#
| 3♠  | S/O, 12--15
| 4♠  | 15+

Opener passed 3♣ on 3 boards, each with 11 HCP and 4--5 clubs.  On 3 more
boards, opener bid 4♣ with 18--19 HCP.

## Strong jump shift 1♥ - 2♠

Opener describes the hand naturally.  2NT shows a minimum balanced hand.

| 1♥ - 2♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 2NT  | 11--15 HCP, BAL, 2--3♠
| 3♣♦  | 4+#
| 3♥   | 6+♥
| 3♠   | 4=♠
| 3NT  | 18--19 HCP, BAL
| 4♣♦! | SPL, **0--1#**, 4=♠

The 2011 writeup lists two 2NT rows here, 12--17 and 18--21 HCP.  Wbridge5
bids 3NT with the strong balanced hand.

## Serious 3NT and splinters

Over [serious 3NT](../1M.md) or a splinter, opener signs off
with a minimum and control-bids otherwise.  A control is an ace, a king, a
singleton, or a void.  The two auctions probed were 1♥ - 3NT and 1♥ - 3♠.

| 1♥ - 3NT!<br>1♥ - 3♠! | |
|------|-|
| 4♣♦! | Control bid, 15+
| 4♥   | S/O, 13--15

## Not probed

- Responder's rebids after 1♥ - 1♠ - 2♦, 2♥, 2♠ or a jump, including
  1♥ - 1♠ - 2♦ - 3♣.
- Opener's rebid after the fourth suit 1♥ - 1♠ - 2♣ - 2♦, and responder's
  call after the Roudy answers.
- Responder's call after game tries other than 1♥ - 2♥ - 3♣, and after
  1♠ - 3♣ - 3♠.
- Opener's rebids after the limit raise 1♠ - 3♦, the preemptive raise
  1M - 3M, the strong jump shift 1♠ - 3♥, the serious 1♠ - 3NT, and the
  splinters other than 1♥ - 3♠.
- P - 1♠ - 2NT, and the fit-showing jumps other than P - 1♥ - 2♠ and
  P - 1♠ - 3♣.
