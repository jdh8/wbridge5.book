# Natural 1♥♠

Modern bidding systems often require 5+ cards to open in a major.  This is
somewhat *conventional* because usually a 4-card suit is biddable in bridge,
but this greatly improves search of 5-3 and 6-2 major fits.

Although minor openings are lower and more frequent, major openings are
explained first because

1. Major openings are more symmetric where both require 5+ cards.
2. There are fewer mediocre exceptional bids which are difficult for beginners.
3. Some conventions designed for majors can also apply to minors.

## Light openings

Opening a major at the last 2 seats requires only 11+ HCP and 5+ cards, or
just 10 HCP with 5-5 shape.

Nevertheless, a weak 2 can be made with only 5 cards at a non-unfavorable 3rd
seat.  This phenomenon excludes Drury from the Wbridge5 bidding system because
major openings at the 3rd seat are seldom light.

## Responses to 1♥

The 1NT response is a [semiforcing](1M/1NT.md) dustbin, and new suits at the
2-level are [forcing 2/1](1M/TwoOverOne.md).  The strength of the preemptive
4♥ [depends on vulnerability](#preemptive-raise-to-the-4-level).

| 1♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♠   | 6--16, 4+♠
| 1NT  | Semiforcing, 6--11 HCP, no other suitable call
| 2♣♦  | 2/1, 12+, 4+#
| 2♥   | 6--10, 3+♥
| 2♠   | SJS, 17+, 4+♠
| 2NT! | [Special 2NT](#special-2nt), 11--16, 3+♥, 2+ cards in each suit
| 3♣!  | Bergen mixed raise, 9--10, 4+♥
| 3♦!  | Bergen LIM, 11--12, 4+♥
| 3♥   | Bergen PRE, 6--8, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, 12--16, **0--1♠**, 4+♥
| 3NT! | Serious 3NT, 16--19, 4+♥, 2--3 cards in each side suit
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 12--16, **0--1#**, 4+♥
| 4♥   | PRE, 0--8<sup>&minus;</sup> HCP, 5+♥

### Adjusted responses by a passed responder

Responses from a passed responder are no longer forcing because the previous
pass has limited the strength.  Some conventions are cancelled to make these
bids passable.

| P - 1♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣♦  | 11--12, 5+#
| 2♠!  | Fit-showing, 10--12, 5+♠, 3+♥
| 3♣♦! | Fit-showing, 10--12, 5+#, 3+♥
| 3♥   | LIM, 10--12, 4+♥, no singleton or void

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that a passed responder still splinters with
shortness, so the limit raise denies it.

## Preemptive raise to the 4-level

Such raise directly jumps to game, showing at most 8<sup>&minus;</sup> HCP and
usually 5+ support.  Its minimum strength is determined by vulnerability.

- **Unfavorable:** 10+
- **Both vulnerable:** 10+
- **None vulnerable:** 8+
- **Favorable:** 7+

The **12 points** are intentionally duplicated in this table.  It is difficult

## Choice of new suit

1. Choose the longest suit.
2. To break a tie, bid the cheapest **call** for 4-4, the highest **suit** for
   5-5 or above.

In the following examples, your partner opened 1♥ and you have not passed.

- **♠J1064 ♥A8 ♦A4 ♣108763:** Bid 1♠.  This hand is too weak to bid 2♣.
- **♠AK3 ♥K6 ♦J652 ♣AK53:** Bid 2♣.  Bid the cheapest call for 4-4.
- **♠Q9 ♥6 ♦Q9743 ♣AK942:** Bid 2♦.  Bid the highest suit for 5-5.
- **♠A103 ♥A953 ♦AQ9 ♣AK3:** Bid 2♣.  This hand is too strong for 3NT.
  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows a 3-card minor bid with such a blockbuster: 2♣
  on 272 of 300 boards and 2♦ on the rest.

## Special 2NT

The special 2NT shows

- Limit raise or better without slam interest (11--16 points, 3+ support).
- Somewhat balanced (2+ cards in every suit).

If responder has passed, this 2NT bid has given enough information for opener
to sign off.

### Puppet special 2NT

If responder has not passed yet, this 2NT bid is a **puppet** forcing opener
to bid an artificial relay 3♣.  Responder shows support and strength in the
second round.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that opener splinters instead of relaying with
17+ points and a singleton or void.  See [constructive
rebids](1M/Rebids.md#unpassed-responder).

The 3♥ response is also forcing to game because Bergen 3♦ already takes care of
invitational hands.

| 1M - 2NT! -<br>3♣! - | |
|------|-|
| 3♦!  | Minimum short, INV+, 11--14, 3=M
| 3♥!  | Minimum long, FG, 12--14, 4+M
| 3♠!  | Maximum long, FG, 14--16, 4+M
| 3NT! | Maximum short, FG, 14--16, 3=M

This convention appears to be Wbridge5's own, probably derived from Jacoby
2NT.  It relieves the 2/1 structure of Wbridge5 in three ways.

- Special 2NT handles 3-card limit raise, freeing dustbin 1NT from forcing.
- Jacoby 2NT fits into special 2NT and serious 3NT, leaving no burden for other
  bids.
- Responder shows.  Opener relays, hiding information from opponents.
