# Strong 2♣

The strong 2♣ shows 21--23 points not suited to a 2NT opening, or 22--23 HCP
with 2NT distribution.  [The openings chapter][open] tells it apart from 1 of a
suit, 2NT, and 2♦.  Opener's rebid is natural, and a 2NT rebid continues
as a 2NT opening.

The 2♣ opening is forcing for one round only.  Responder never passed 2♣ in
the probes, but after 2♣ - 2♦ responder may pass a simple rebid with a bust.

Every row in this chapter comes from probing Wbridge5 5.12, which shows that
2♣ is opened with 19--23 HCP.  Ranges in HCP are as probed; the program
counts length and shortness as well (see [hand evaluation](Evaluation.md)), so
shapely hands go positive with fewer HCP.

Vulnerability hardly matters.  At unfavorable vulnerability, the same 300
hands with 19--26 HCP made the same openings, and 300 responders with 6--12
HCP made the same responses at unfavorable and both vulnerable.  The few
changes are in responder's and opener's game decisions below.

[open]: Openings.md#strong-2--2-openings

## Responses

| 2♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♦! | Waiting, usually 0--9 HCP, any hand without a positive
| 2♥♠ | 5+#, 10+ points, usually 7+ HCP
| 2NT | 10+ HCP, no better positive
| 3♣♦ | 6+# with 2 of the top 3 honors, 10+ points
| 3♥♠ | 7+# with 2 of the top 3 honors

The positive suit responses are by points.  A 5-card major goes positive at
about 10 points counting shortness and length, with as few as 7 HCP, while a
flat 10 HCP hand has to choose between 2♦ and 2NT.

A 6-card minor needs quality for a 3-level response, as the official help
says: 2 of the top 3 honors.  A poorer 6-card minor responds 2NT with 10+ HCP,
or 2♦ with less.  2NT may also hold a 5-card major: 33 of 132 hands with
10--13 HCP and 5+♥ responded 2NT.

- **♠92 ♥AQ10864 ♦Q64 ♣108:** 2♥.  Only 8 HCP but 6 hearts.
- **♠86 ♥A10653 ♦K965 ♣103:** 2♦.  7 HCP and two doubletons fall short of
  10 points.
- **♠Q2 ♥J753 ♦J ♣AKJ872:** 3♣ with a good suit.
- **♠10 ♥842 ♦AKQ ♣Q106542:** 2NT.  The clubs are too poor for 3♣.

A strong responder makes the same positive as a minimum one.  There is no
jump response for strength, and 2♦ was never bid with 16+ HCP.  Of 300 hands
with 16--24 HCP, 187 responded 2NT, 47 2♠, 38 2♥, 13 3♣, 9 3♦, 4 3♠, 1 3♥,
and 1 7♥.  The rules above hold at 16--22 HCP.  A 5-card major bids 2♥ or 2♠
(256 and 258 of 300 hands), or 2NT with a poor suit holding at most one of
the top 3 honors.  A 6-card minor bids 3♣ or 3♦ (267 and 275 of 300), or 2NT
with only one of the top 3 honors.  A 7-card major with 2 of the top 3
honors bids 3♥ or 3♠ (14 hands each).  The only slam jumps were 2 grand slams
with a long suit and a void.

- **♠AK ♥AK3 ♦K76 ♣Q10432:** 2NT with 19 HCP.
- **♠K10964 ♥KQ2 ♦AKJ ♣108:** 2NT.  The spades are too poor for 2♠.
- **♠A5 ♥AKQ8432 ♦— ♣AKJ8:** 7♥.
- **♠— ♥A2 ♦AQJ97543 ♣AQJ:** 7♦.

### Responses over interference

Over an overcall, pass is weak and double shows values without a natural
call.  The double is not penalty; it is the usual call when the overcall took
responder's own suit.

| 2♣ - (2♥) - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 0--7 HCP
| X   | 8+ HCP, no ♥ stopper or length in ♥
| 2NT | 10+ HCP, ♥ stopper
| 2♠  | 5+♠, 10+ points
| 3♣♦ | 6+#

- **♠8732 ♥96 ♦K85 ♣KQJ7:** Double.  9 HCP, no heart stopper.
- **♠92 ♥AQ10864 ♦Q64 ♣108:** Double.  The 2♥ response was taken away.
- **♠AK8 ♥K1073 ♦KQ932 ♣9:** 2NT.

A takeout double of 2♣ hardly changes Wbridge5's responses.  It bids 2♦ with
most hands, including 42 of the 57 hands that would respond 2♥ or 2♠.  The
probes never saw pass or redouble.

| 2♣ - (X) - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♦  | Most hands, including positives with a major
| 2NT | 10+ HCP, about half of the hands that would bid 2NT undisturbed
| 3♣♦ | As without the double

## Opener's rebids

### After 2♣ - 2♦

| 2♣ - 2♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥♠ | 5+#
| 2NT | 21--23 HCP, BAL, 5m-4-2-2, or 4-4-4-1
| 3♣♦ | 6+#

The 2NT rebid is a [delayed 2NT opening][delay], mostly with 22--23 HCP.
Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows 14 of 62 such rebids with 21 HCP, and some
4-4-4-1 hands with a small singleton.

Rarer rebids:

- **3♠ or 4♥:** 7+ cards in the major (1 and 2 hands).
- **3NT:** 5-5 in the majors (3 of the 4 such hands).  The fourth rebid 2NT.

[delay]: 1NT.md#delayed-2nt-openings

For example,

- **♠AK ♥Q1073 ♦AK107 ♣AK5:** 2NT.
- **♠A4 ♥K10854 ♦A10 ♣AKQ4:** 2♥.
- **♠AK2 ♥QJ ♦KQ10952 ♣AQ:** 3♦.

seen.

### Over interference

Opener's double after 2♣ - 2♦ is takeout and shows a 4-4-4-1, as the 2011
writeup says.  Pass shows a balanced hand without a stopper.  Partner has
another chance to act.

| 2♣ - 2♦ -<br>(2♥) - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 21--23 HCP, BAL, no ♥ stopper
| X   | 4-4-4-1, singleton ♥
| 2♠  | 5+♠
| 2NT | ♥ stopper, including hands with 5+♥
| 3♣  | 6+♣
| 3♦  | 5+♦

When the overcall is passed around, the meanings change: double is the
balanced hand without a stopper, and pass is a penalty pass with hearts.

| 2♣ - (2♥) - P -<br>(P) - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 5+♥
| X   | 21--23 HCP, BAL, no ♥ stopper
| 2NT | ♥ stopper
| 2♠  | 5+♠
| 3♣♦ | 5+#

- **♠AQ7 ♥653 ♦AKQ6 ♣AK7:** Pass over 2♣ - 2♦ - (2♥), but double over
  2♣ - (2♥) - P - (P).
- **♠AK42 ♥A ♦AQJ4 ♣A876:** Double over 2♣ - 2♦ - (2♥), but 2NT over
  2♣ - (2♥) - P - (P).
- **♠Q ♥AK10876 ♦KJ ♣KQJ7:** Pass over 2♣ - (2♥) - P - (P).

Vulnerability does not change this table.  At unfavorable and at favorable
vulnerability, 2 and 1 of the same 245 calls differed from none vulnerable.
The penalty pass came up on 28--30 boards each time.

### After 2♣ - 2♦ - 2NT

Opener's 2NT rebid shows a 2NT opening with about 2 HCP more, and
Wbridge5 plays [systems on](Glossary.md#systems-on): Stayman, transfers, and
their answers are those [after a 2NT opening](1NT.md#responses-to-2nt).
Only responder's ranges move down by 2 HCP.

| 3NT  | 3--8 HCP, no 4-card major
| 4♣!  | 6+♦
