# Opposing strong openings

This chapter covers Wbridge5's calls after a strong opening at the 2 level.

- **(2♣)** is strong and artificial in all three systems Wbridge5 plays: its
  own, SEF, and SAYC.
- **(2♦)** is a strong artificial game force in Wbridge5's own system and
  in SEF.  SAYC opens a weak 2♦.
- **Natural strong (2♦♥♠)** belong to no preset system.  They are a Wbridge5
  option that the opponents may choose by turning weak twos off.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that its calls over (2♣) do not change when the
opponents play the SAYC preset, nor over (2♦) when they play SEF: the same
60 hands drew the same 60 calls.

All probes were at love all.  Ranges are in HCP as probed unless marked as
points (see [hand evaluation](../Evaluation.md)).

## Over a strong (2♣♦)

| (2♣♦)  | |
|-------|-|
| X!    | 5+♥, 5+♠, about 9+ points
| 2♦♥♠  | NAT, 5+#, or 6+# with a weak hand
| 2NT!  | 5+♦, 5+♣, about 11+ points
| 3♣♦   | NAT, 6+#, about 12+ points
| Jumps | Preemptive

These calls are the same over both openings.  A double is the only way to
show both majors.

- **Double:** It is played from 5--6 HCP up, and has no upper limit.  On the
  probes, 5-5 majors with 6--10 HCP doubled (2♣) 163 times and passed 8
  times.  With 1--5 HCP, Wbridge5 usually passes.
- **Simple overcalls:** A 5-card major overcalls with about 11 points,
  sometimes 9.  Over (2♣), a 5-card diamond suit needs 12+ HCP for 2♦.  A 6-card
  major overcalls at the 2 level with 5--11 HCP, much like a weak two.
- **Unusual notrump:** 2NT shows the minors.  With 5-5 minors, Wbridge5 bids
  2NT on 10+ HCP and sometimes on 8--9 HCP.  Weaker hands pass.
- **Minors at the 3 level:** 3♣ over (2♣) and 3♣♦ over (2♦) are natural
  6-card suits with about 12+ points.  A 5-card minor passes; Wbridge5 never
  overcalled one at the 3 level.
- **Jumps:** A jump to 3 of a major takes a 6-card suit with two of the top
  three honors, or a 7-card suit.  A jump to 4 of a major takes a 7-card
  suit.  (2♣) 3♦ and (2♣) 4♣ are 7+-card preempts with 6--10 HCP.  These
  are the criteria of [preemptive openings](../Openings/Preemptive.md).

There is no natural notrump and no takeout double.  A strong balanced hand
passes.  All 27 hands with 15--18 HCP and no 5-card suit passed (2♣),
such as ♠AK3 ♥K6 ♦J652 ♣AK53.

- **♠AJ862 ♥KQ942 ♦4 ♣92:** Double.
- **♠Q9853 ♥Q9865 ♦J4 ♣2:** Pass.  This hand is too weak to double.
- **♠KQ9762 ♥10 ♦K9 ♣K843:** Bid 2♠.
- **♠AQJ763 ♥J ♦32 ♣10852:** Bid 3♠.
- **♠9 ♥82 ♦A10764 ♣A10752:** Bid 2NT.
- **♠Q2 ♥J753 ♦J ♣AKJ872:** Bid 3♣ over (2♣).

## After the artificial response

Once responder has made the artificial response, a double of it shows that
suit.  The majors double is gone.

| (2♣) - P - (2♦) | |
|-------|-|
| X     | 5+♦, about 11+ points, or 6+♦ with 6--10 HCP
| 2♥♠   | NAT, 5+#
| 2NT!  | 5+♦, 5+♣
| 3♣    | NAT, 6+♣
| 3♦    | NAT, 6+♦, about 10+ HCP
| Jumps | Preemptive

| (2♦) - P - (2♥) | |
|------|-|
| X    | 5+♥, from about 8 HCP
| 2♠   | NAT, 5+♠
| 2NT! | 5+♦, 5+♣
| 3♣♦  | NAT, 6+#
| 3♥   | NAT, 6+♥, 9+ HCP

Here 2♥ is the no-ace response; see [strong 2♦](../2D.md).  The other calls
are the same as over the opening itself.  After (2♣) P (2♦), Wbridge5 bids a
5-5 major two-suiter as one major: 2♠ on 166 of 300 hands and 2♥ on 58.
Most of the rest passed.

- **♠AK8 ♥K1073 ♦KQ932 ♣9:** Double (2♣) P (2♦).
- **♠J2 ♥54 ♦AK109854 ♣Q10:** Bid 3♦ over (2♣) P (2♦).
- **♠92 ♥AQ10864 ♦Q64 ♣108:** Double (2♦) P (2♥).

## Natural strong twos

When weak twos are off, the opponents' 2♦♥♠ openings are natural and strong.
The opponents' strong-2♦ setting then has no effect.  When Wbridge5 itself
plays strong twos, responder answers 2♥ with a 2NT negative on 3--6 HCP and
passes with 0--3 HCP, so the opening is not absolutely forcing.  The calls
in the balancing seat below come from this case.

### Over (2♥♠)

| (2♥♠) | |
|------|-|
| X    | Takeout, short in their suit, about 13+ points; or 17+ HCP
| 2♠   | NAT, 5+♠, about 11+ points
| 2NT  | NAT, 16--20 HCP, BAL
| 3♣♦  | NAT, 6+#, 9--16 HCP
| 3♥   | NAT over (2♠), 5+♥, about 13+ points
| 4NT! | 5+♦, 5+♣, about 16+ points

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that 2NT is natural here, not unusual.  The
minors take 4NT instead, and weaker 5-5 minors pass or bid one minor at the 3
level.  The cuebid was never seen as Michaels: with 5-5 spades and a minor,
Wbridge5 bids 2♠ over (2♥) or doubles with 14+ HCP.

With 4 spades and a singleton heart, a takeout double can be as light as
10 HCP, as with ♠Q963 ♥7 ♦AJ76 ♣QJ83.  With 2+ hearts it takes about 13 HCP.
A 6-card heart suit bids 3♥ over (2♠) from about 9 HCP.

- **♠KJ ♥K72 ♦KQ74 ♣KQJ10:** Bid 2NT.
- **♠K3 ♥KQJ62 ♦10 ♣AJ865:** Bid 3♥ over (2♠).
- **♠K8 ♥4 ♦K10943 ♣AKJ84:** Bid 4NT over (2♥).

### Over a natural (2♦)

| (2♦)  | |
|------|-|
| X    | 14--20 HCP, usually balanced
| 2♥♠  | NAT, 5+#, about 11+ points
| 2NT! | 5+♦, 5+♣
| 3♣   | NAT, 6+♣, 12--16 HCP
| 3♦!  | 5+♥, 5+♠

Over a natural (2♦), 2NT still shows the minors, so a strong balanced hand
doubles.  A diamond suit passes, even with 6+ cards and 15 HCP.  In a
20-hand probe, 5-5 majors split evenly: 3♦ with 9--15 HCP, and a double
with 8--9 or 17+ HCP.  The main probe has one 3♦, ♠AJ862 ♥KQ942 ♦4 ♣92.

### Sandwich and balancing over (2♥)

| (2♥) - P - (2NT) | |
|------|-|
| X    | 15--18 HCP
| 3♣♦♠ | NAT, 6+#, 10+ HCP

After the 2NT negative, Wbridge5 passes most hands up to 16 HCP.

| (2♥) - P - (P) | |
|-----|-|
| X   | Takeout or strong, 11--20 HCP
| 2♠  | NAT, 5+♠ (once 4), 7--15 HCP
| 2NT | NAT, 13--18 HCP
| 3♣♦ | NAT, 5+#, 7--15 HCP

In the balancing seat, every call needs a few HCP less than in the direct
seat.  A 5-card minor is enough at the 3 level, as with ♠K ♥K95 ♦AQJ95
♣10865 (3♦).

## Not probed

- **Advances:** advancer's calls after any of these overcalls were not
  probed, including whether a natural 2NT over (2♥♠) is
  [systems on](../Glossary.md#systems-on).
- **Other auctions:** natural (2♠) and (2♦) were probed only in the direct
  seat; (2♥) was also probed after (P) (2NT) and (P) (P).  After (2♦) P
  (2♥), 5-5 majors were not probed.
- **Thin rows:** jumps over natural strong twos (one or two rows each), and
  3♦ with the majors over a natural (2♦) outside that 20-hand probe.
- **SAYC's 2♦:** it is a weak two and belongs with weak openings.
- **Doubles of a strong 2NT:** the help file says they show both majors.
