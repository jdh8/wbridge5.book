# Natural 1NT overcall

Wbridge5 has three natural 1NT overcalls of a 1-of-a-suit opening.  The seat
and whether the overcaller has passed decide which one it is.

| Auction | |
|------------------|-|
| (1y) 1NT         | 15--18 HCP, 1NT distribution, stopper in the adverse suit
| (1y) P (P) 1NT   | 12--14 HCP, BAL
| P (1y) P (P) 1NT | 10--11 HCP, BAL, by a passed hand

A passed hand's direct 1NT is [unusual](1y.md#unusual-notrump), not one of
these.  In the balancing seat a passed hand's 1NT is natural again.

The balancing ranges come from probing Wbridge5 5.12.  Over (1♥) P (P),
balanced hands with 12--14 HCP bid 1NT and those with 15+ HCP mostly
double.  Only 2 of the 17 hands with 15 HCP bid 1NT.  The range is the same
over (1♣), (1♦) and (1♠).  Over the minors, balanced hands with 12--14 HCP
and no 5-card major bid 1NT on 86 of 144 boards and otherwise doubled, passed
or bid a suit, and 7 of 35 balanced hands with 15 HCP bid 1NT over the three
openings.  When the balancer has passed before, it bids 1NT with 10--11 HCP.

After every one of these overcalls, the advancer plays
[systems on](../Glossary.md#systems-on).  Its calls are the
[responses to a 1NT opening](../1NT.md#responses-to-1nt), with
[Stayman](../1NT/2C.md) and [transfers](../1NT/Transfers.md).  The changes
come from the adverse suit and from the overcaller's range.

The probes were run with no one vulnerable.  Advancer held 0--16 HCP.
Ranges here are in HCP.

## Advancing the direct 1NT

### Over (1♣♦)

The probes dealt the same hands to the advancer and to a responder after
1NT - (P).  Over (1♣), advancer made the same call on 265 of 283 hands, and
over (1♦) on 264 of 283.  Wbridge5 drops the transfer into the adverse
minor.

| (1♣) 1NT (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | S/T, 5+♦, 5+♣; no transfer to clubs

| (1♦) 1NT (P) | |
|----|-|
| 3♣ | Not bid; no transfer to diamonds

A weak hand with 6+ cards in the adverse minor passes, e.g. ♠A75 ♥7 ♦874
♣QJ6532 over (1♣) and ♠86 ♥2 ♦Q108753 ♣QJ53 over (1♦).  The second hand
transfers with 3♣ over (1♥).  Stronger hands with the adverse minor bid
2NT or 3NT.

### Over (1♥♠)

Over a major, advancer made the same call as a 1NT responder on 191 (1♥) and
199 (1♠) of 283 hands.  Most changes come from holding length in the adverse
major.

| (1♥) 1NT (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 2♣! | STAY, 4=♠, 7+ HCP
| 2♥! | TRF, 5+♠, any strength; also 5-5 in the majors
| 2NT | INV, 7--9 HCP, mostly 8
| 3♣! | TRF, 6+♦
| 3♦♠ | SJS, 5+#, usually 12+ HCP
| 3♥! | 0--1♥, 4+ in each other suit
| 3NT | S/O, 8--15 HCP

| (1♠) 1NT (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 2♣! | STAY, 4=♥, 7+ HCP
| 2♦! | TRF, 5+♥, any strength; also 5-5 in the majors
| 2NT | INV, 7--9 HCP
| 3♣! | TRF, 6+♦
| 3♦♥ | SJS, 5+#, usually 12+ HCP
| 3♠! | 0--1♠, 4+ in each other suit
| 3NT | S/O, 8--15 HCP

Other calls are as over a 1NT opening, e.g. 2♠ for clubs and 4♠ for the
minors.

- **Stayman asks only for the other major.**  Without 4 cards in it,
  advancer bids 2NT or 3NT, even with 4 cards in the adverse major.  Over
  (1♥), ♠73 ♥AJ75 ♦J1062 ♣AJ6 bids 3NT and ♠Q3 ♥Q982 ♦A10983 ♣74 bids
  2NT.  After a 1NT opening, both bid 2♣.
- **No transfer to the adverse major.**  A weak hand with 5+ cards in it
  passes, e.g. ♠86 ♥A10653 ♦K965 ♣103 over (1♥).  A better one bids 2NT or
  3NT: over (1♠), ♠Q10964 ♥AQ10 ♦6 ♣10743 bids 3NT.
- **5-5 majors transfer to the other major**, e.g. ♠AJ862 ♥KQ942 ♦4 ♣92
  bids 2♥ over (1♥) and 2♦ over (1♠).  After a 1NT opening this hand bids 4♦.
- **The jump cuebid shows a 3-suiter** short in the adverse major.  It is
  rare (2 boards over (1♥), 3 over (1♠)), 8--15 HCP, e.g. ♠K1093 ♥A ♦K874
  ♣KQ62 bids 3♥.

The bid just below the adverse major, (1♥) 2♦ or (1♠) 2♥, is natural.
Advancer almost never makes it: 2 of 300 boards with 5+♥ over (1♥) and 2 of
300 with 5+♠ over (1♠).  Each of those hands has 5 cards in the adverse major
and a 6-card minor.  The overcaller always reads it as natural
([below](#overcallers-rebids)).

## Overcaller's rebids

When Wbridge5 overcalls 1NT itself, it answers advancer as a 1NT opener
does, with two changes:

- Stayman asks for the other major only.
- The bid just below the adverse major is a natural sign-off.

| (1♥) 1NT (P) 2♣ (P) | |
|----|-|
| 2♦ | 0--3♠, even with 4♥
| 2♠ | 4=♠

| (1♣) 1NT (P) 2♥ (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠  | Acceptance
| 2NT | MAX, 17--18 HCP, 3=♠
| 3♠  | Super acceptance, 4=♠, 16--18 HCP but once 15

| (1♥) 1NT (P) 2♦ (P) | |
|---|-|
| P | Always (171 of 171)

| (1♠) 1NT (P) 2♥ (P) | |
|----|-|
| P  | Default (170 of 172)
| 3♥ | 18 HCP, 4=♥

For example, ♠J5 ♥AK63 ♦KQ108 ♣K96 answers 2♦ over Stayman after (1♥).
♠KQJ ♥1082 ♦K542 ♣AKJ bids 2NT over 2♥, and ♠AJ98 ♥A5 ♦K65 ♣A764 bids 3♠.
These answers fit the [transfer rebids](../1NT/Transfers.md#1nt-openers-rebids).

## Responder raises: (1♥) 1NT (2♥)

When responder raises the adverse suit, advancer bids as after
[1NT (2♥)](../1NT/Competition.md#responses-over-2-of-a-suit), with one
exception: it never doubles.  The probe dealt the same hands to both
auctions.  Advancer made the same call on 237 of 283, and none of the 300
boards drew a double.  The hands that make a cooperative double after
1NT (2♥) bid 3NT, 2NT or pass instead.  For example, ♠KQ4 ♥842 ♦1093 ♣AJ72
bids 3NT and ♠8732 ♥96 ♦K85 ♣KQJ7 bids 2NT.

| (1♥) 1NT (2♥) | |
|------|-|
| X    | Not bid
| 2♠   | S/O, 3--7 HCP, 5+♠
| 2NT  | 8--9 HCP, as LEB after 1NT (2♥)
| 3♣♦♠ | 5+#, 7+ HCP
| 3♥!  | 4=♠, 1=♥, 4=♦, 4=♣
| 3NT  | S/O, 8--16 HCP
| 4♠   | 6--8 HCP, 6+♠

## Advancing a balancing 1NT

After (1♥) P (P) 1NT (P), advancer has passed over the opening.  It keeps
the 1NT system but needs about 2 HCP more than over a direct 1NT.  On the same
hands as over a direct (1♥) 1NT, it passed 82 hands that would have bid
there.

| (1♥) P (P) 1NT (P) | |
|------|-|
| P    | Up to 10 HCP
| 2♣!  | STAY, 10--16 HCP, 4=♠
| 2♥!  | TRF, 2--7 HCP, 5♠
| 2♠!  | TRF, 1--8 HCP, 6+♣
| 2NT  | INV, 9--11 HCP
| 3♣!  | TRF, 5--8 HCP, 6♦
| 3♦♠! | Takeout, 8--13 HCP, 0--1#, 4+ in each other suit
| 3NT  | S/O, 10+ HCP

Balanced hands usually pass with 9--10 HCP and bid at 11 HCP.  For example,
♠KQ4 ♥842 ♦1093 ♣AJ72 passes, though it bids 3NT over the direct overcall.
♠A1053 ♥J1052 ♦A2 ♣K74 bids 2♣.

The jump shifts are takeout jump shifts, as for a
[passed responder](../1NT.md#takeout-jump-shifts).  For example, ♠— ♥Q732
♦A10762 ♣Q1082 bids 3♠.

### After a passed hand's balancing 1NT

After P (1♥) P (P) 1NT (P), advancer has also passed over the opening, and
partner's 1NT shows only 10--11 HCP.  The thresholds rise again.  The probe
dealt advancer at most 12 HCP.

| P (1♥) P (P) 1NT (P) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | Up to 11 HCP
| 2♣! | STAY, 11--12 HCP, 4=♠
| 2♥! | TRF, 3--7 HCP, 5♠
| 2♠! | TRF, 3--8 HCP, 6+♣
| 2NT | INV, 11--12 HCP
| 3♣! | TRF, 5--8 HCP, 6♦
| 3♠! | Takeout, 8--11 HCP, 0--1♠

Compared with the unpassed balancing 1NT, 11--12 HCP hands invite instead of
bidding game: ♠73 ♥AJ75 ♦J1062 ♣AJ6 bids 2NT here and 3NT there.

## Not probed

- Stoppers in the adverse suit.  The harness constrains only suit lengths.
  The probed overcallers held 3--4 cards in it.
- Advancer's second round, e.g. after a Stayman answer or an acceptance.
- Overcaller's rebids after the balancing 1NTs.
- Advancer with 17+ HCP: the 4NT and 6NT ranges stop at the probe's 16 HCP cap.
- Overcaller's answer to 2NT over (1♥) 1NT (2♥).
- Interference other than (1♥) 1NT (2♥), e.g. a double or a new suit.
- Advancing a balancing 1NT over openings other than (1♥), and a passed
  hand's balancing 1NT over them.
