# Responsive doubles

When partner makes a takeout double and RHO raises opener's suit or bids a
new suit, advancer's double is **responsive**.  It shows 4+ cards in both
unbid suits and leaves the choice to the doubler.  After partner's simple
overcall, Wbridge5 has no responsive double.

The official help lists only the negative and support doubles as doubles
that are not for penalty.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows responsive doubles as
well.  Opener and RHO in these probes played Wbridge5's own system, at no
vulnerability.  Ranges are in points, except where marked HCP.  Unless noted,
advancer held 4--14 HCP.

## After a raise

Over (1♣) X (2♣), the double shows both majors.  A hand with one 4-card major
bids it.  The probe gave advancer 3+ cards in each major.

| (1♣) X (2♣) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 4--9 HCP
| X!  | Responsive, 7--16, 4+♠, 4+♥
| 2♦  | 6--11, 5+♦
| 2♥♠ | 5--13, 4+#, usually the only 4-card major
| 2NT | 10--14 HCP
| 3♣! | Cuebid, 12--14 HCP
| 3♦  | INV, 10--13, 5+♦
| 3♥♠ | INV, 11--18, 5+#
| 3NT | 13--14 HCP
| 4♣! | 12--15, 5+♠, 5+♥
| 4♥♠ | 13--16, 6+#

Of the 69 hands with 4+ cards in both majors, 59 doubled.  Four passed with
4--6 HCP.  The 4♣ row comes from 3 boards.

- **♠AQ97 ♥A763 ♦1093 ♣94:** Double (1♣) X (2♣).
- **♠10876 ♥AJ3 ♦Q1094 ♣Q5:** Bid 2♠ over (1♣) X (2♣).

Over (1♥) X (2♥), the double shows both minors.  A 4-card spade suit takes
precedence.  The probe gave advancer 3+ cards in each minor.

| (1♥) X (2♥) | |
|------|-|
| P    | 4--11 HCP
| X!   | Responsive, 8--16, 4+♦, 4+♣, 0--3♠
| 2♠   | 6--14, 4+♠
| 2NT  | 10--14 HCP
| 3♣♦  | 8--15, 5+#
| 3♥!  | Cuebid, 12+

Of the 76 hands with 4+ cards in both minors, 56 doubled.  Six passed with
4--6 HCP, and the 5-5 hands bid 4NT.  A broader probe, with any shape and
0--12 HCP, gave the same double on 23 boards, all with 4+ cards in both
minors and 0--3 spades.

- **♠J104 ♥QJ ♦KQ65 ♣J982:** Double (1♥) X (2♥).
- **♠K ♥K95 ♦AQJ95 ♣10865:** Double (1♥) X (2♥).

## After a new suit

Over (1♣) X (1♠), the double shows the red suits.  With 5+ hearts and a weak
hand, advancer bids 2♥ instead.  The probe gave advancer 0--3 spades.

| X!  | Responsive, 8--15, 4+♥, 4+♦

| 3♥  | INV, 10--13, 5+♥

Of the 16 hands with exactly 4 hearts, 4+ diamonds and 4--10 HCP, 13
doubled.

- **♠874 ♥A953 ♦A964 ♣93:** Double (1♣) X (1♠).

Over (1♥) X (1♠), the double shows both minors in the same way.  A broad
probe with 0--12 HCP gave it on 27 boards, all with 4+ cards in both minors,
4--12 HCP and 8--14 points.  The 7 hands of that shape that passed held 1--6
HCP.

## Other doubles by advancer

The same broad probes, with 0--12 HCP, show two more doubles.

- **(1♥) X (1NT):** The double shows 10--12 HCP and any shape (49 boards).
  It is a value double, not a responsive one.
- **(1♥) X (2NT):** The double shows 7--12 HCP (16 boards) with no common
  shape.

## After an overcall

Over (1♣) 1♥ (2♣), advancer raises with 3 hearts, bids a 5-card suit, or
passes.  The double appeared only on 2 boards, each with 14 HCP, 4 spades
and 2 hearts.

| (1♣) 1♥ (2♣) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | Most hands with 0--2♥
| X   | 14 HCP, 4=♠, 2=♥
| 2♦  | 10--17, 5+♦
| 2♥  | 4--15, 3=♥
| 2♠  | 10--17, 5+♠
| 3♣! | Cuebid, 12--14 HCP
| 4♠  | 17--18, 6+♠

Of the 34 hands with 4 spades and 4+ diamonds, 14 raised hearts with 3
cards, 11 passed, and 2 doubled.  The probe gave advancer 0--3 hearts.

## Not probed

- Vulnerability: every probe was at none vulnerable.
- Advancer with 15+ HCP, and (1♦) X (2♦) or (1♠) X (2♠).
- Responsive doubles at the 3-level, e.g. (1♥) X (3♥).
- The doubler's rebid after a responsive double.
- Advancer's double when RHO bids a new suit over partner's overcall, and
  over (1M) overcall (2M).
