# Second responses

This page covers responder's second call after opener's 1NT rebid or a new
suit at the 1-level.  Opener's rebids are on the pages of the
[1♦ response](1D.md) and the [1♥♠ responses](1M.md).

## After a 1NT rebid

Opener's 1NT shows 12--14 HCP, balanced or 5-4-2-2, and 2--3 cards in a major
responded.  Responder can sign off in 2 of a 5-card major with a weak hand.
Stronger hands with 5+ cards in the responded suit start with
[Roudy](../Glossary.md#roudy) 2♣.

### Responded a major

|-----|-|
| P   | 6--10 HCP
| 2♣! | Roudy, 10+, 5+M

| 2M  | S/O, 8--11, 5+M

| 3M  | INV, 10--12, 6+M

After 1♦ - 1M - 1NT, a 2♦ preference shows 6--10 HCP and 3+ diamonds.  After
1♠, 5-5 majors with 11--13 HCP jump to 3♥.

- **♠96542 ♥KQJ2 ♦10 ♣J42:** Bid 2♥ over 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT.
- **♠K8653 ♥AKJ2 ♦10 ♣874:** Bid 2♣ over 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT.  This is Roudy.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows 2♣ in 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT also from 4♠ and 4♣ with 8--10
HCP.  What responder does after opener's answer was not probed.

### Answering Roudy

Opener answers in the **short-long-long-short** coding of
[Roudy](../Glossary.md#roudy).  The answer is about responder's suit, so
2♥ shows 3-card support for spades in 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT - 2♣.  A maximum with
3-card support and 5 clubs bids 3♣, so 2♠ has only 4 clubs after a 1♣
opening.

| 1m - 1X - 1NT -<br>2♣! - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | 11--13 HCP, 2=X
| 2♥!  | 11--13 HCP, 3=X
| 2♠!  | 13--14 HCP, 3=X
| 2NT! | 13--14 HCP, 2=X
| 3♣   | 12--14 HCP, 3=X, 5♣

- **♠1032 ♥J10 ♦AK6 ♣AJ983:** Bid 2♦ over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1NT - 2♣.
- **♠63 ♥AK2 ♦954 ♣KQ543:** Bid 2♥ over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1NT - 2♣.
- **♠K84 ♥Q104 ♦KQ9 ♣A842:** Bid 2♠ over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1NT - 2♣.
- **♠K82 ♥A2 ♦KJ9 ♣K10983:** Bid 2NT over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1NT - 2♣.
- **♠82 ♥Q96 ♦K97 ♣AKQ94:** Bid 3♣ over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1NT - 2♣.

The help file also lists 3♦ for a maximum with 3-card support and 5
diamonds.  The probes held no such hand.

## Fourth suit

A fourth suit is forcing only when it is 2♣ or 2♦.  Others are natural.  In
particular, 1♠ in 1♣ - 1♦ - 1♥ - 1♠ shows 4 spades.

### After 1m - 1♥ - 1♠

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 6--8 HCP, 3♠
| 1NT | 7--10 HCP
| 2♣  | 6--9 HCP, 4+♣
| 2♦! | FSF, 11+
| 2♥  | S/O, 6--9 HCP, 6+♥
| 2NT | INV, 10--12 HCP
| 3♣  | INV, 11--14, 4♥, 5+♣
| 3♥  | INV, 11--14, 6+♥
| 3NT | 13--15 HCP

After 1♦ - 1♥ - 1♠, 2♣ is the fourth suit.  A 2♦ preference shows 6--10 HCP and
3+ diamonds, and 3♦ invites with 4+ diamonds.

- **♠J53 ♥AJ532 ♦K94 ♣K10:** Bid 2♦ over 1♣ - 1♥ - 1♠.
