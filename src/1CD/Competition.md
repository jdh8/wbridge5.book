# Competitive bidding

## Responses after a takeout double

The takeout double shows opening values, so responder should bid more
preemptively.  There are some adjustments to the basic structure.

Playing 2NT is infeasible now as 1♣xx is more profitable.  As a result, the
meaning of 2NT is changed to a convention.  For its original meaning, redouble
instead.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that 3NT over 1♣ (X) is still natural.

Jump shifts become **weak** if responder unpassed.  Redouble to show strength
for original strong jump shifts.  Keep in mind that **fit-showing** by a passed
responder are unadjusted because *the previous **pass denies both** jump
shifts*.

### Responses to 1♣ (Dbl)

A simple raise can now be made with only 5 points.  The jump shifts are
**weak** for an unpassed responder.

| 1♣ (X) | |
|------|-|
| XX   | INV+, 11+, not a Truscott 2NT
| 2♣   | 5--10, 4+♣
| 2♥♠  | **WJS**, 8--11, 6+#
| 2NT! | Truscott, CONST as former 3♣
| 3♣   | PRE, 7--10, 5+♣
| 3NT  | 13--15 HCP, BAL, 5+♣

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows no weak 2♦.  A weak hand with 6+ diamonds bids 1♦.

A passed responder keeps the **fit-showing** jumps unchanged.

| P - 1♣ (X) | |
|------|-|
| 2♥♠! | fit-showing, 11+, 5+#, 5+♣
| 3♦!  | fit-showing, 11+, 5+♦, 5+♣

### Responses to 1♦ (Dbl)

A simple raise can now be made with only 5 points.  The jump shifts are
**weak** for an unpassed responder.

| 1♦ (X) | |
|------|-|
| XX   | INV+, 11+, no other suitable call
| 2♣   | INV+, 12+, 4+♣
| 2♦   | 5--10, 4+♦
| 2♥♠  | **WJS**, 8--11, 6+#
| 2NT! | Truscott, CONST as former 3♦
| 3♣   | **WJS**, 8+, 6+♣
| 3♦   | PRE, 6--9, 5+♦

A passed responder keeps the **fit-showing** jumps unchanged.

| P - 1♦ (X) | |
|------|-|
| 2♥♠! | fit-showing, 11+, 5+#, 4+♦
| 3♣!  | fit-showing, 11+, 5+♣, 4+♦

## Responses after a simple suit overcall

Wbridge5 employs French convention for such low-level competitive auctions.
The French insist on showing length of hearts quickly to compete with
latent adverse spades.

### Collante

The French word *collante* means “sticky” in English.  This means that a
negative double after a 1♦ or 1♥ overcall shows exactly 4 cards in **the next
suit** *viz* ♥ or ♠ respectively.  As a consequence, the direct bid of the suit
now shows 5+ cards.

Be aware that Collante only affects one suit.  The meaning of 1♠ in 1♣ (1♦) 1♠
is the same as 1♣ - 1♠, which shows 4+ cards in spades, because only hearts is
affected by (1♦).

| 1m (1♦) | |
|----|-|
| X  | NEG, 6+, 4=♥
| 1♥ | free bid, 6+, 5+♥
| 1♠ | free bid, 6+, 4+♠
| 2♦ | cuebid, 14+ HCP

| 1m (1♥) | |
|----|-|
| X  | NEG, 6+, 4=♠
| 1♠ | free bid, 6+, 5+♠
| 2♥ | cuebid, 13+ HCP

### Rodrigue

This convention is uncommon in France, but is used by Wbridge5.  After a 1♠
overcall, we give up the simple raise for a negative free bid of hearts.  This
bid differentiates with the negative double by showing 5+ cards of hearts,
making the negative double show exactly 4 cards.

Wbridge5 madly insists on showing length of hearts quickly to compete with
latent adverse spades.

| 1m (1♠) | |
|-----|-|
| X   | NEG, 6+, 4=♥
| 2m! | NEG free bid, 6--10, 5+♥
| 2♥  | (positive) free bid, 11+, 5+♥
| 2♠  | cuebid, 13+ HCP
| 3m  | mixed raise, 9--13, 5+m

## Opener's rebids after a negative double

Responder's calls mean the same with or without LHO's overcall, so opener
rebids as in an [uncontested auction](1M.md#openers-rebids).  The negative
double stands for a response in the major it shows.  Since the double shows
**exactly 4 cards**, opener raises only with 4-card support.

### Over 1♣ (1♦) Dbl

Opener raises as over 1♣ - 1♥, but the cheap 1♥ is now available for a
minimum raise.  Each raise therefore shows a little more.

| 1♣ (1♦) X (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 1♥  | 13--15, 4♥
| 1♠  | 13--18, 4♠, 0--3♥
| 1NT | 12--14 HCP, BAL, 2--3♥
| 2♣  | 13--18, 5+♣, UNBAL
| 2♥  | 14--16, 4♥
| 2♠  | 18+, 4♠, 5+♣
| 2NT | 17--21 HCP, BAL
| 3♣  | 17--20, 6+♣
| 3♥  | INV, 16--20, 4♥
| 4♥  | 17--18 HCP, 4♥, 5-4-2-2

With 4 hearts and a singleton, a strong opener can also make a double jump in
the short suit to show a splinter (3♠, 4♦) or jump to 4♣ with 6 clubs.

- **♠94 ♥Q732 ♦KQ ♣AJ1094:** Bid 1♥ over 1♣ (1♦) X.  This is a minimum raise.
- **♠K10 ♥Q962 ♦93 ♣AKQ97:** Bid 2♥ over 1♣ (1♦) X.
- **♠A8 ♥KJ83 ♦4 ♣KQJ654:** Bid 3♥ over 1♣ (1♦) X.

### Over 1m (1♥) Dbl

The double shows exactly 4 spades.  The spade raises step up the same way.

| 1m (1♥) X (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 1♠  | 12--15, 4♠
| 1NT | 12--15 HCP, BAL, 2--3♠
| 2m  | 13--17, 5+m, UNBAL
| 2♠  | 14--17, 4♠
| 2NT | 17--21 HCP, BAL
| 3m  | 17--20, 6+m
| 3♠  | INV, 15--20, 4♠

### Over 1m (1♠) Dbl

The double shows exactly 4 hearts.  There is no room for a 1♥ raise, so the
raises are the same as over 1m - 1♥.

| 1m (1♠) X (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 1NT | 12--14 HCP, BAL
| 2♣  | 13--18, 5+♣
| 2♥  | 12--16, 4♥
| 2NT | 17--21 HCP, BAL
| 3♥  | INV, 17--19, 4♥

## Opener's rebids after Rodrigue

Opener supports hearts with only 2 cards because [Rodrigue](#rodrigue) shows
5+ hearts.  Opener passes with a singleton heart or a long minor in a minimum.

| 1m (1♠) 2m! (P) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 13--17, (6+m or 0--1♥)
| 2♥  | 12--15, 2+♥
| 2♠! | cuebid, 18+, 4♥
| 3m  | 16--20, 6+m
| 3♥  | INV, 14--18, 3+♥
| 4♥  | 17+, 3+♥

- **♠Q742 ♥Q ♦AQ3 ♣Q9632:** Pass 1♣ (1♠) 2♣ with a singleton heart.
- **♠KJ6 ♥J3 ♦Q ♣AQ107542:** Pass 1♣ (1♠) 2♣.  Clubs are a good enough
  contract.

If RHO competes, opener's 3♥ is competitive.

| 1♣ (1♠) 2♣! (2♠) | |
|----|-|
| P  | 13--15, usually 2♥
| 3♣ | 14--18, 6+♣
| 3♥ | 12--16, 3+♥
| 4♥ | 15+, 3+♥

## Opener's rebids after a free bid in a major

After [Collante](#collante) a free 1♥ shows 5+ hearts, and after 1♥ overcall
a free 1♠ shows 5+ spades.  Opener raises with 3 cards.  These raises do not
change when RHO acts.

| 1♣ (1♦) 1♥ (X) | |
|-----|-|
| XX! | 15+, 4♠, 2=♥
| 1♠  | 13--17, 4♠, 0--2♥
| 2♣  | 13--17, 5+♣
| 2♥  | 12--16, 3+♥
| 3♦! | SPL, 15--17, 3+♥, 0--1♦
| 3♥  | INV, 16--21, 3+♥
| 4♥  | 18+, 3+♥

The 2011 writeup calls this redouble a support redouble with 2+ hearts and 4+
spades.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows exactly 2 hearts, since opener simply
raises with 3.

| 1♣ (1♦) 1♥ (2♦) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 13--15, 0--2♥
| X!  | 15, 4♠, 2=♥
| 2♥  | 12--15, 3+♥
| 2♠  | 16+, 4♠, 0--2♥
| 3♣  | 14+, 6+♣
| 3♥  | INV, 15--20, 3+♥
| 3NT | 16--18 HCP, BAL, 2♥
| 4♦! | SPL, 17+, 4♥, 0--1♦

The 2011 writeup requires 16+ for this double.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows it
with 13 HCP, as below.  Stronger hands with 4 spades bid 2♠.

- **♠AQ94 ♥KJ ♦95 ♣K7542:** Redouble 1♣ (1♦) 1♥ (X), or double 1♣ (1♦) 1♥
  (2♦).

| 1♣ (1♥) 1♠ (2♥) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 13--16, 0--2♠
| 2♠  | 12--16, 3+♠
| 3♣  | 14+, 6+♣
| 3♠  | INV, 15--19, 3+♠
| 3NT | 17--19 HCP, 2♠
| 4♣! | fit-showing, 17+, 3+♠, 6♣
| 4♠  | 18+, 3+♠

## Opener's rebids after a takeout double

### After a redouble

When RHO passes, opener always passes the redouble and lets responder
describe.  When RHO bids, opener's double shows 3+ cards in RHO's suit or a
strong hand.

| 1♣ (X) XX (1♠) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 12--15, minimum
| X   | 14+, (3+♠ or 17+ HCP)
| 1NT | 12--14 HCP, BAL
| 2♣  | 13--18, 6+♣
| 2♥  | 15--19, 4♥, UNBAL

### After Truscott 2NT

The [Truscott 2NT](#responses-after-a-takeout-double) is a constructive
raise.  Opener signs off at 3m with a minimum and bids game otherwise.

| 1m (X) 2NT! (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 3m  | S/O, 11--13 HCP
| 3NT | 13+ HCP, BAL
| 4m  | 15+ HCP, slam interest
| 5m  | 14+, UNBAL

- **♠94 ♥Q732 ♦KQ ♣AJ1094:** Bid 3♣ over 1♣ (X) 2NT.
- **♠Q4 ♥AKJ3 ♦2 ♣KQ10983:** Bid 5♣ over 1♣ (X) 2NT.

### After a weak jump shift

Opener raises with 2 cards because the weak jump shift shows 6+ cards.

| 1m (X) 2M (P) | |
|---------------------------|-|
| P                         | 13--14 HCP
| 3m                        | 15+, 6+m, 0--1M
| 3M                        | 14--18, 2+M
| 4m                        | fit-showing, 17+, 6+m, 3+M
| Other jump to the 4-level | SPL, 17+, 3+M, 0--1#
| 4M                        | 17+, 2+M

Over 2♥, a 2♠ rebid shows 4 spades, a singleton heart, and 19+.

- **♠AKQ10 ♥972 ♦Q2 ♣K1087:** Bid 3♥ over 1♣ (X) 2♥.
- **♠AQ94 ♥KJ ♦95 ♣K7542:** Pass 1♣ (X) 2♥.

## Opener's rebids after responder's cuebid

After a cuebid, opener rebids naturally.

| 1♣ (1♦) 2♦ (P) | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥♠ | 4+#
| 2NT | 12--14 HCP, BAL
| 3♣  | 5+♣
| 3NT | 15+ HCP, BAL

## Maximal double

The double is a game try when we have found a major fit, both opponents have
bid, and 3 of our major would only be competitive.

| 1♣ (1♥) 1♠ (2♥)<br>2♠ (3♥) | |
|-----|-|
| 3♠  | 8--10, 5+♠
| X!  | Game try, 11--12, 5+♠
| 4♥! | cuebid, 13+, 5+♠, 0--1♥
| 4♠  | 13+, 5+♠

- **♠AJ963 ♥6 ♦1053 ♣A532:** Double.
- **♠KQJ98 ♥10 ♦94 ♣J10532:** Bid 3♠.
