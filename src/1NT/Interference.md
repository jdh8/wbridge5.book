# Responses overcalled

This chapter covers 1NT - 2♣ (Stayman) or a transfer, then RHO doubles or
overcalls before opener rebids.  Competition directly over 1NT is in
[its own chapter](Competition.md).

Wbridge5 follows two principles here.

- **A double leaves room, so systems are on.**  Opener gives the
  undisturbed answer ([systems on](../Glossary.md#systems-on)), and the
  redouble and the pass take the hands that have nothing to show.
- **An overcall takes away room.**  Opener shows the fit as cheaply as it can
  and doubles mainly with length in the overcalled suit.  Some doubles over
  Stayman show shape instead.

The probes dealt opener 15--17 HCP with 2--5 cards in every suit at no
vulnerability.  A spot check over 1NT - 2♣ (2♥) with both sides vulnerable
gave the same calls.  The range "points" means HCP plus 1 point per
doubleton, as in [Hand evaluation](../Evaluation.md).

## Stayman doubled

| 1NT - 2♣ (X) | |
|------|-|
| XX   | 0--3♥, 0--3♠, ♣ stopped
| 2♦!  | 0--3♥, 0--3♠, ♣ unstopped
| 2♥   | 4=♥, 0--3♠
| 2♠   | 4=♠, 0--3♥
| 2NT! | 4=♠, 4=♥
| 3♣!  | MIN, 5=♥
| 3♦!  | MIN, 5=♠
| 3♥   | MAX, 5=♥
| 3♠   | MAX, 5=♠

Apart from XX and 2♦, these are the [undisturbed answers](2C.md#openers-rebids).
The major answers ignore the club stopper.  Opener never passes.

- **♠AK2 ♥Q3 ♦Q7532 ♣KJ6:** XX.
- **♠A64 ♥AJ7 ♦KQ542 ♣Q10:** 2♦, clubs unstopped.
- **♠A85 ♥KQ65 ♦KQJ7 ♣83:** 2♥, even without a club stopper.

**Adjunct:** Opener passes with clubs unstopped and redoubles without a
4-card major but with clubs worth playing.  Other answers are undisturbed and
promise a club stopper.  After the pass, responder's XX repeats Stayman.

## Stayman overcalled

### 1NT - 2♣ (2♦)

Opener answers Stayman as undisturbed but shows a 5-card major at the 3
level at any strength.  Without a 4-card major, the double mostly shows 3-3 in
the majors, so responder can still bid a 5-card major.  With 2-2 in the
majors, it usually doubles with 5 diamonds and passes with 5 clubs.  The
double does not depend on a diamond stopper.

| 1NT - 2♣ (2♦) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 0--3♥, 0--3♠, a doubleton major
| X   | 3=♥, 3=♠ or (2=♥, 2=♠, 5♦)
| 2♥  | 4=♥, 0--3♠
| 2♠  | 4=♠, 0--3♥
| 2NT | 4=♠, 4=♥
| 3♥  | 5=♥
| 3♠  | 5=♠

- **♠KQJ ♥1082 ♦K542 ♣AKJ:** X.
- **♠AK2 ♥Q3 ♦Q7532 ♣KJ6:** Pass, although diamonds are stopped.

### 1NT - 2♣ (2♥)

Opener usually passes without 4 spades, even with 4 or 5 hearts.  Wbridge5
doubles or bids 2NT only with 4+ hearts and a stopper, and only sometimes.
The data shows no strength or shape rule that separates these three calls.

| 1NT - 2♣ (2♥) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 0--3♠
| X   | PEN, 4+♥, 0--3♠
| 2♠  | 4=♠
| 2NT | NAT, 4+♥, ♥ stopped, 0--3♠
| 3♠  | 5=♠

- **♠J4 ♥AK95 ♦92 ♣AQJ92:** X.
- **♠A72 ♥K985 ♦K10 ♣AQ76:** 2NT.
- **♠J5 ♥AK63 ♦KQ108 ♣K96:** Pass.

### 1NT - 2♣ (2♠)

| 1NT - 2♣ (2♠) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 0--3♥
| X   | PEN, 4+♠, 0--3♥
| 2NT | NAT, 4+♠, ♠ stopped, 0--3♥
| 3♥  | 4+♥

- **♠AQ64 ♥AJ ♦J8543 ♣A6:** X.
- **♠AK9 ♥10932 ♦AJ ♣QJ85:** 3♥.

**Adjunct:** X shows 4 hearts and a spade stopper, and 3♥ shows 4 hearts
without one.

### 1NT - 2♣ (3♦)

With 4+ diamonds, opener doubles even with a 4-card major.  With 3 diamonds
and a major, it either doubles or bids the major.

| 1NT - 2♣ (3♦) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 0--3♥, 0--3♠, ♦ short or unstopped
| X   | 3+♦, ♦ stopped
| 3♥  | 4+♥, 0--3♠, 2--3♦
| 3♠  | 4+♠, 0--3♥, 2--3♦
| 3NT | 4=♠, 4=♥, 2--3♦

- **♠AQ64 ♥AJ ♦J8543 ♣A6:** X.
- **♠AKQ2 ♥AQ108 ♦J7 ♣973:** 3NT.
- **♠KQ6 ♥AK3 ♦J6 ♣KJ963:** Pass.

### 1NT - 2♣ (3♠)

Without 4 hearts, the double is [cooperative](Competition/X.md).  Opener
passes mostly with 15 HCP and a doubleton spade.

| 1NT - 2♣ (3♠) | |
|----|-|
| P  | MIN, usually 2♠, 0--3♥
| X  | Cooperative, 0--3♥
| 4♥ | 4+♥

- **♠AK ♥K32 ♦A74 ♣Q10764:** X.
- **♠J2 ♥AK8 ♦Q82 ♣AJ932:** Pass.

## Transfer doubled

Opener sorts hands first by support, then by a stopper in the doubled suit.
Here M is the major that responder has shown.

| 1NT - 2♦♥ (X) | |
|-----|-|
| P   | 2=M, doubled suit unstopped
| XX  | 2=M, doubled suit stopped
| 2M  | 3--4M, 15--16 points
| 2NT | 17 HCP, 3=M or 4-3-3-3
| 3M  | 4+M, 17+ points

Opener jumps to 3M with every 5-card fit.  Over 2♦ (X), opener with 5
spades and 2 hearts bids 2♠ instead of passing or redoubling.

The jump needs less than an
[undisturbed super acceptance](Transfers.md#1nt-openers-rebids).  With 4 hearts,
16 HCP and a doubleton, opener jumps.  With 4-3-3-3 and the same 16 HCP, it
bids only 2♥.

- **♠AQ64 ♥AJ ♦J8543 ♣A6:** XX over 2♦ (X).
- **♠AQ54 ♥J3 ♦64 ♣AKQ62:** Pass over 2♦ (X).
- **♠AK9 ♥10932 ♦AJ ♣QJ85:** 2♥ over 2♦ (X).
- **♠J5 ♥AK63 ♦KQ108 ♣K96:** 3♥ over 2♦ (X).
- **♠KQ6 ♥AK3 ♦J6 ♣KJ963:** 2NT over 2♦ (X).
- **♠AQ987 ♥109 ♦AJ6 ♣AJ9:** 2♠ over 2♦ (X).

## Transfer overcalled

### Overcall in responder's major

The overcall of 2♥ over 2♦, or 2♠ over 2♥, takes away the acceptance.
The double replaces it.

| 1NT - 2♦ (2♥)<br>1NT - 2♥ (2♠) | |
|----|-|
| P  | 2=M
| X  | 3--4M, 15--16 points
| 3M | 4+M, 17+ points

- **♠AK2 ♥Q3 ♦Q7532 ♣KJ6:** X over 2♥ (2♠).
- **♠AQ64 ♥AJ ♦J8543 ♣A6:** 3♠ over 2♥ (2♠).

### Other overcalls

Opener accepts at the 3 level with any 3-card support.  With a doubleton,
opener doubles a 3-level overcall when holding 4+ cards in that suit.
Opener never doubles 1NT - 2♦ (2♠), even with 4--5 spades.

| 1NT - 2♦ (2♠ 3♣)<br>1NT - 2♥ (3♣ 3♦) | |
|----|-|
| P  | 2=M
| X  | PEN, 2=M, 4+ in the overcalled suit, 3-level only
| 3M | 3+M

- **♠AQ54 ♥J3 ♦64 ♣AKQ62:** X over 2♦ (3♣), but Pass over 2♦ (2♠).
- **♠AK9 ♥10932 ♦AJ ♣QJ85:** 3♥ over 2♦ (2♠).

## Minor transfers

### 1NT - 2♠ (X)

| 1NT - 2♠ (X) | |
|-----|-|
| XX  | 2=♣, ♠ stopped
| 2NT | 16--17 HCP, 4+♣
| 3♣  | Acceptance

The acceptance includes doubleton clubs with spades unstopped.  The 2NT
super acceptance is the [undisturbed one](Transfers.md#1nt-openers-rebids).

- **♠AQ64 ♥AJ ♦J8543 ♣A6:** XX.
- **♠KQ6 ♥AK3 ♦J6 ♣KJ963:** 2NT.

### 1NT - 2♠ (3♣ 3♦)

| 1NT - 2♠ (3♣ 3♦) | |
|---|-|
| P | Default
| X | PEN, 5♣ over 3♣, 4+♦ over 3♦

### 1NT - 3♣ (X)

Opener always relays 3♦, the
[undisturbed rebid](Transfers.md#transfer-to-diamonds-3).

## Not probed

- Responder's continuations, e.g. after opener passes a doubled transfer.
- Overcalls of 2NT, 3♣, or 3♥ over Stayman, and overcalls over 1NT - 3♣.
- An overcall in responder's artificial suit, e.g. 1NT - 2♦ (3♦).
- Vulnerability beyond the single spot check above.
