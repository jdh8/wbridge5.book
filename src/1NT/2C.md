# Stayman

Stayman is the most used relay in bridge.  This convention helps find 4-4 fit
in a major.

The *vanilla* Stayman requires 9+ points to 1NT and 6+ points to 2NT in order
to safely sign off at the next notrump when no major fit is found.  This also
implies an exactly 4-card major and denies a 6-card major.  Transfer with a
6-card major because a fit is already found.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows no Stayman with 4-3-3-3 below 12 HCP, e.g.
♠AK87 ♥1072 ♦842 ♣Q97 raises 1NT to 3NT.

Stayman is unlimited and gives no information about minors.  It is possible
that responder pursues 6NT or bids a 5-card minor in later rounds.

## Garbage Stayman

As the most frequent rebids are simple ♠, ♥, and ♦, responder can psych a
Stayman with a ♠-♥-♦ *3-suiter* with **insufficient strength**.  Such a
legitimized psych is Garbage Stayman.

Criteria for a *3-suiter* are less strict here.  The 5-4-3-1 pattern also
counts as a *3-suiter*, which ironically provides 56% of hands to this
convention.  However, a 5-card major is unacceptable because a transfer scores
more.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows Garbage Stayman on every 0--7 HCP hand with
3--4♠, 3--4♥ and 0--1♣, vulnerable or not, down to 0 HCP.  The exception is
6+♦, where responder transfers with 3♣ instead.  With 2--3 clubs, responder
passes 1NT.  4-4 majors take Garbage Stayman with a singleton or void club,
e.g. 4-4-4-1, but usually pass with 2+ clubs.  Weak 5-4 majors transfer, and
use Stayman only from 6--7 HCP for the invitational 2♥ or 2♠ over 2♦.

A 1NT opening is assumed in the following examples.

- **♠Q83 ♥10984 ♦J10743 ♣2:** Off-shape Garbage Stayman.
- **♠QJ52 ♥10975 ♦8632 ♣4:** In-shape Garbage Stayman.
- **♠J532 ♥108764 ♦AJ7 ♣7:** Bid ♦ to transfer to hearts.

## Opener's rebids

A 5-card major is allowed as a 1NT opener in Wbridge5.  Hence, there are
5-card major rebids after 1NT - 2♣.

| 1NT - 2♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | 0--3♥, 0--3♠
| 2♥   | 4=♥, 0--3♠
| 2♠   | 4=♠, 0--3♥
| 2NT! | 4=♠, 4=♥
| 3♣!  | MIN, 5=♥
| 3♦!  | MIN, 5=♠
| 3♥   | MAX, 5=♥
| 3♠   | MAX, 5=♠

The notrump response showing majors is French standard but unexpected outside.
Therefore, it requires an alert in most countries.

A 2NT opener rarely has a 5-card major and rebids it as a 4-card one.  The
rebids after 2NT - 3♣ are just one level up from 1NT - 2♣.

## Second responses to a 1NT opener

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2♦

Opener denies a 4-card major.  As a consequence, the only possible major fits
are 3-5, so a second response should show 5 cards.  A 5-card major implies 4
cards in the other major as Stayman requires an exactly 4-card major.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2♦ - | |
|---------|-|
| P       | Garbage Stayman
| 2♥♠     | INV+, 5=#, 4=OM
| 3♣♦     | FG, 5+#
| Notrump | NAT, strength as without Stayman

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows 2♥ from 7 HCP and 2♠ from 6 HCP.  Opener never
passes 2♥: it bids 2NT with a doubleton heart, 3♥ with three hearts at 15--16
HCP, or 4♥ with three hearts at any strength.  Over 2NT, responder passes at
6--8 HCP and bids 3NT at 7--9 HCP.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2♥

Opener's major rebid denies the other major as in the French system.  Responder
immediately knows if there is any major fit.  Opener can infer exactly 4 cards
in the other major from a **misfit** second response.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | Garbage Stayman
| 2NT  | INV, 0--3♥
| 3♣♦  | FG, 5+#, 0--3♥
| 3♥   | INV, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, S/T, 14+, 0--1♠, 4+♥
| 3NT  | S/O, 0--3♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, S/T, 14+, 0--1#, 4+♥
| 4♥   | S/O, 4+♥
| 4NT! | RKCB 0314, no singleton or void
| 6NT  | S/O, 0--3♥
| 7NT  | S/O, 0--3♥

Wbridge5 does not use 2♠ here.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 with 4+ cards in opener's major shows that every
Splinter held its singleton or void, and no 4NT held a singleton or void.
After 2♥, 4NT came from 27 hands with 14--23 HCP.  After 2♠, it came from 18
hands with 15--21 HCP.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that opener answers 4NT as
[RKCB 0314](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314) in hearts, counting the ♥K, on all 287
boards.  For example, ♠A85 ♥KQ65 ♦KQJ7 ♣83 bids 5♠, with 2 keys and the queen.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2♠

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2♠ - | |
|-------|-|
| P     | Garbage Stayman
| 2NT   | INV, 0--3♠
| 3♣♦   | FG, 5+#, 0--3♠
| 3♥    | FG, 13+ HCP, 4+♥, 4+♠
| 3♠    | INV, 4+♠
| 3NT   | S/O, 0--3♠
| 4♣♦♥! | SPL, S/T, 14+, 0--1#, 4+♠
| 4♠    | S/O, 4+♠
| 4NT!  | RKCB 0314, no singleton or void
| 6NT   | S/O, 0--3♠
| 7NT   | S/O, 0--3♠

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows Garbage Stayman passing 2♥ or 2♠ unless it
holds 4 cards in opener's major at 7 HCP, and now and then at 6 HCP.  Then it
invites with 3♥ or 3♠, or bids game at 7 HCP with 5--6 diamonds.  ♠J92 ♥Q986
♦A7652 ♣8 raises 2♥ to 3♥.  A 6-card diamond suit at 6--7 HCP sometimes bids
3♦.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2NT

Opener's notrump rebid shows both majors.  As a major fit is found, there is no
need to find a minor fit.  Minor bids are South African transfers, and the
natural major bids are invitational.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows the transfers from 6 HCP up to 20 HCP, while 3♥
and 3♠ take every weaker hand, 0--9 HCP.  Garbage Stayman bids 3♥ or 3♠ here
on most boards, and transfers only now and then at 6--7 HCP.  With 4-4 in
majors, responder transfers to hearts or bids 3♥.  ♠J943 ♥A653 ♦J654 ♣10
bids 3♥, and ♠Q962 ♥532 ♦KJ543 ♣6 bids 3♠.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣! | TRF, 4+♥
| 3♦! | TRF, 4+♠
| 3♥♠ | NF, 0--9 HCP, 4+#

### 1NT - 2♣ - 3♣

Because opener has shown strength, quantitative invitation is no longer needed.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>3♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♦  | FG, 5+♦, 0--2♥
| 3♥  | S/O, 3+♥
| 3NT | S/O, 0--2♥
| 4♣  | S/T, 6+♣, 0--2♥
| 4♥  | S/O, 3+♥

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows no RKCB here.  Responder bids 4♥ even with 16--20
HCP.  Garbage Stayman bids 3♥, or 4♥ with 4 hearts at 6--7 HCP.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 3♦

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>3♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 3♠   | S/O, 3+♠
| 3NT  | S/O, 0--2♠
| 4♣♦  | FG, 6+#, 0--2♠
| 4♠   | S/O, 3+♠
| 4NT! | RKCB 0314

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows Garbage Stayman bidding 3♠, or 4♠ with 4 spades
at 6--7 HCP (46 of 97 such hands), as over 3♣.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 3♥

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>3♥ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | Garbage Stayman
| 3NT  | S/O, 0--2♥
| 4♣♦  | S/T, 5+#, 0--2♥
| 4♥   | S/O, 3+♥
| 4NT! | RKCB 0314

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows Garbage Stayman passing with three hearts up to
5 HCP and raising to 4♥ at 6--7 HCP about half the time.  With four hearts, it
raises from 4--5 HCP up.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 3♠

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>3♠ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | Garbage Stayman
| 3NT  | S/O, 0--2♠
| 4♣♦  | S/T, 5+#, 0--2♠
| 4♠   | S/O, 3+♠
| 4NT! | RKCB 0314

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows Garbage Stayman passing with three spades up to 5
HCP and raising to 4♠ at 6--7 HCP about half the time (20 of 42).  With four
spades, it raises from 4--5 HCP up (39 of 57 there, all 97 at 6--7 HCP), as
over 3♥.

## Second responses to a 2NT opener

The 2NT opener has significantly more points, and a *vanilla* Stayman requires
game-forcing strength now.  There are some adjustments to reflect this change.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3♦

| 2NT - 3♣ -<br>3♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | Garbage Stayman, 5+♦
| 3♥! | Smolen, 5=♠
| 3♠! | Smolen, 5=♥
| 4♣♦ | S/T, 5+#
| 3NT | S/O, 3--10 HCP
| 6NT | S/O, 11--15 HCP
| 7NT | S/O, 16+ HCP

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that responder seldom invites with 4NT here,
unlike a direct response to 2NT.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 also shows Garbage Stayman over 2NT only at 3--4 HCP.
Weaker garbage hands pass 2NT, and 6+♦ transfers with 4♣.  After 3♦, these
3--4 HCP hands usually bid 3NT, e.g. ♠Q104 ♥J1064 ♦98765 ♣9.  Only 19 of 116
passed, all with 5--6 diamonds.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3♥♠

Structure is unchanged from 1NT - 2♣ - 2♥♠.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3NT

There is no invitation here.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows the South African
transfers at any strength, 4--20 HCP, and direct 4♠ only rarely.  With 4-4 in
majors, responder transfers to hearts.

| 2NT - 3♣ -<br>3NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 4♣! | TRF, 4+♥
| 4♦! | TRF, 4+♠
| 4♥♠ | S/O, 4+#

## Not probed

- 2NT - 3♣ - 3♥ or 3♠.
- Opener's third call other than over 1NT - 2♣ - 2♦ - 2♥ and 1NT - 2♣ - 2♥ -
  4NT, e.g. the answer to 4NT after 2♠, 3♦, 3♥ or 3♠, or to Smolen.
- 1NT - 2♣ - 3♦ - 4♦ and 2NT - 3♣ - 3NT - 4♥: no board.
