# Stayman

Stayman is the most used relay in bridge.  This convention helps find 4-4 fit
in a major.

The *vanilla* Stayman requires 9+ points to 1NT and 6+ points to 2NT in order
to safely sign off at the next notrump when no major fit is found.  This also
implies an exactly 4-card major and denies a 6-card major.  Transfer with a
6-card major because a fit is already found.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows no Stayman with 4-3-3-3 below 12 HCP, e.g.
♠AK87 ♥1072 ♦842 ♣Q97 raises 1NT to 3NT.

Stayman is unlimited and gives no information about minors.  It is possible
that responder pursues 6NT or bids a 5-card minor in later rounds.

## Garbage Stayman

As the most frequent rebids are simple ♠, ♥, and ♦, responder can psych a
Stayman with a ♠-♥-♦ *3-suiter* with **insufficient strength**.  Such a
legitimized psych is Garbage Stayman.

Criteria for a *3-suiter* are less strict here.  The 5-4-3-1 pattern also
counts as a *3-suiter*, which ironically provides 56% of hands to this
convention.  However, a 5-card major is unacceptable because a transfer scores
more.

A 1NT opening is assumed in the following examples.

- **♠Q83 ♥10984 ♦J10743 ♣2:** Off-shape Garbage Stayman.
- **♠QJ52 ♥10975 ♦8632 ♣4:** In-shape Garbage Stayman.
- **♠J532 ♥108764 ♦AJ7 ♣7:** Bid ♦ to transfer to hearts.

## Opener's rebids

A 5-card major is allowed as a 1NT opener in Wbridge5.  Hence, there are
5-card major rebids after 1NT - 2♣.

| 1NT - 2♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | 0--3♥, 0--3♠
| 2♥   | 4=♥, 0--3♠
| 2♠   | 4=♠, 0--3♥
| 2NT! | 4=♠, 4=♥
| 3♣!  | MIN, 5=♥
| 3♦!  | MIN, 5=♠
| 3♥   | MAX, 5=♥
| 3♠   | MAX, 5=♠

The notrump response showing majors is French standard but unexpected outside.
Therefore, it requires an alert in most countries.

A 2NT opener rarely has a 5-card major and rebids it as a 4-card one.  The
rebids after 2NT - 3♣ are just one level up from 1NT - 2♣.

## Second responses to a 1NT opener

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2♦

Opener denies a 4-card major.  As a consequence, the only possible major fits
are 3-5, so a second response should show 5 cards.  A 5-card major implies 4
cards in the other major as Stayman requires an exactly 4-card major.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2♦ - | |
|---------|-|
| P       | Garbage Stayman
| 2♥♠     | INV+, 5=#, 4=OM
| 3♣♦     | FG, 5+#
| Notrump | NAT, strength as without Stayman

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2♥

Opener's major rebid denies the other major as in the French system.  Responder
immediately knows if there is any major fit.  Opener can infer exactly 4 cards
in the other major from a **misfit** second response.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | Garbage Stayman
| 2NT  | INV, 0--3♥
| 3♣♦  | FG, 5+#, 0--3♥
| 3♥   | INV, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, S/T, 14+, 0--1♠, 4+♥
| 3NT  | S/O, 0--3♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, S/T, 14+, 0--1#, 4+♥
| 4♥   | S/O, 4+♥
| 4NT! | RKCB 0314
| 6NT  | S/O, 0--3♥
| 7NT  | S/O, 0--3♥

Wbridge5 does not use 2♠ here.

### 1NT - 2♣ - 2NT

Opener's notrump rebid shows both majors.  As a major fit is found, there is no
need to find a minor fit.  Minor bids are South African transfers, and the
natural major bids are invitational.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows the transfers at any strength, 7--20 HCP, while
3♥ and 3♠ are only 8--9 HCP.  With 4-4 in majors, responder transfers to
hearts.

| 1NT - 2♣ -<br>2NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣! | TRF, 4+♥
| 3♦! | TRF, 4+♠
| 3♥♠ | INV, 8--9 HCP, 4+#

### 1NT - 2♣ - 3♣

| 4♣  | S/T, 6+♣, 0--2♥
| 4♥  | S/O, 3+♥

## Second responses to a 2NT opener

The 2NT opener has significantly more points, and a *vanilla* Stayman requires
game-forcing strength now.  There are some adjustments to reflect this change.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3♦

| 2NT - 3♣ -<br>3♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| P   | Garbage Stayman
| 3♥! | Smolen, 5=♠
| 3♠! | Smolen, 5=♥
| 4♣♦ | S/T, 5+#
| 3NT | S/O, 4--10 HCP
| 6NT | S/O, 11--15 HCP
| 7NT | S/O, 16+ HCP

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that responder seldom invites with 4NT here,
unlike a direct response to 2NT.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3♥♠

Structure is unchanged from 1NT - 2♣ - 2♥♠.

### 2NT - 3♣ - 3NT

There is no invitation here.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows the South African
transfers at any strength, 4--20 HCP, and direct 4♠ only rarely.  With 4-4 in
majors, responder transfers to hearts.

| 2NT - 3♣ -<br>3NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 4♣! | TRF, 4+♥
| 4♦! | TRF, 4+♠
| 4♥♠ | S/O, 4+#
