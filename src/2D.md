# Strong 2♦

The strong 2♦ is forcing to game.  See
[Openings](Openings.md#strong-2--2-openings) for when to open it.  Besides
balanced blockbusters, Wbridge5 opens 2♦ with 19--22 HCP and a long suit,
such as ♠A5 ♥AKQ8432 ♦- ♣AKJ8.

Since game is certain, the first question is slam.  Responder shows aces right
away, and opener is the *captain*.

## Responses

| 2♦ - | |
|-------|-|
| 2♥!   | No ace, at most one king, 0--8 HCP
| 2♠!   | One ace
| 2NT!  | No ace, (two kings or 9+ HCP)
| 3♣♦♥♠ | No ace, 6+#, KQ or KJ10 in the suit
| 3NT!  | Two aces
| 4♣!   | Three aces

Strength does not matter once responder holds an ace.  The 2♠ response covers
4--18 HCP in the probes, and 3NT covers 8--22 HCP.

The 2011 writeup puts the 2♥ / 2NT boundary at 8 points.  Probing Wbridge5
5.12 shows that 8 HCP with one king usually bids 2♥, as with ♠KQ86 ♥72 ♦Q76
♣J854.  From 9 HCP on, a hand with no ace bids 2NT, as with ♠J104 ♥QJ ♦KQ65
♣J982.

A suit response needs a good suit but little else.  It takes priority over
2NT, holding up to 12 HCP.

- **♠K7 ♥10987 ♦J109 ♣QJ98:** Bid 2♥.  One king is not two.
- **♠82 ♥A53 ♦K943 ♣10532:** Bid 2♠ with any one ace.
- **♠K105 ♥1087 ♦K10532 ♣96:** Bid 2NT with two kings.
- **♠Q83 ♥KQ8763 ♦63 ♣64:** Bid 3♥.
- **♠A6 ♥A103 ♦J8752 ♣J75:** Bid 3NT.
- **♠A754 ♥A986 ♦873 ♣A2:** Bid 4♣.

There is no response for four aces.  Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that it
passes 2♦ with all four aces, as with ♠AJ ♥A87 ♦AKJ ♣AK962 (3 of 3 boards).

A few hands with one ace and a solid 7-card suit jump to the 4-level instead,
as with ♠93 ♥AKQJ975 ♦10 ♣874 bidding 4♥.  Such a 4♣ would clash with the
three-ace response, and only one board showed it.

## Opener's rebids

Opener describes shape naturally.  All suit rebids show 5+ cards.

### Over 2♥ or 2♠

The rebids are the same after either response.

| 2♦ - 2♥/♠ - | |
|------------|-|
| 2NT        | 23+ HCP, no 5-card major nor 6-card minor
| New suit   | 5+#
| Jump in ♥♠ | 7+#, usually 19--21 HCP

The 2NT rebid includes 4-4-4-1 and a 5-card minor.  The 2♠ rebid over 2♥ and
the 3♠ rebid over 2♠ are both the cheapest spade bid.

A 7-card major usually jumps with 19--21 HCP.  Stronger hands with 7 cards,
and some minimum ones, bid the major at the cheapest level.  Minors never
jump.

- **♠AJ ♥A87 ♦AKJ ♣AK962:** Bid 2NT.
- **♠AKQJ9 ♥- ♦AJ10 ♣AKQ106:** Bid 2♠.
- **♠A ♥AKQJ643 ♦Q ♣AK104:** Bid 3♥ over 2♥.
- **♠K7 ♥AKQJ632 ♦AQ4 ♣A:** Bid 4♥ over 2♥.
- **♠AK ♥7 ♦AK5 ♣AQ109642:** Bid 3♣ over 2♠.  Minors do not jump.

**Adjunct:** 3NT shows 5-5 or longer in the majors.  Wbridge5 opens such hands
2♣ instead, even with 24 HCP, so it never faces this rebid.

#### Responder's second round

After the 2NT rebid, **systems are on**.  Responder bids as over a [2NT
opening](1NT.md#responses-to-2nt), taking opener's extra 4 HCP or so into
account.
See [delayed 2NT *openings*](1NT.md#delayed-2nt-openings) and [systems
on](Glossary.md#systems-on).

- **2♦ - 2♥ - 2NT:** 3♣ Stayman, 3♦♥ transfers, 3♠ for clubs, 4♣ for
  diamonds, and 4♠ for 5-5 minors, all with 0--5 HCP.  3NT is to play.
- **2♦ - 2♠ - 2NT:** The same, and the notrump raises are 3NT with 4--6 HCP,
  4NT with 6--10 HCP, and 5NT with 11 HCP.

After a suit rebid over 2♥, responder is very weak.

| 2♦ - 2♥ -<br>2♠ - | |
|-------|-|
| 2NT   | 0--2♠, no 5-card ♥
| 3♣♦♥  | 5+#, 0--2♠
| 3♠    | 3+♠, mostly 5 HCP
| 4♣♦♥! | SPL, 4+♠, 0--1#
| 4♠    | 3+♠

- **♠KQ4 ♥9 ♦6432 ♣87432:** Bid 3♠ over 2♠.
- **♠J9752 ♥954 ♦KJ73 ♣4:** Bid 4♣ over 2♠.
- **♠10853 ♥987 ♦82 ♣J753:** Bid 4♠ over 2♠.

| 2♦ - 2♥ -<br>3♣ - | |
|------|-|
| Pass | 1--3 HCP, 3+♣
| 3♦   | 5+♦, 0--2♣
| 3♥♠  | 4+#
| 3NT  | Usually BAL
| 4♣   | 3+♣, usually with a singleton or void
| 5♣   | 3+♣, usually without a singleton or void

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that the 2♦ opening is not absolutely forcing to
game.  Responder passes 3♣ with a bust and club tolerance, as with ♠J654 ♥542
♦53 ♣9853 (9 of 250 boards).

After a suit rebid over 2♠, responder with one ace and a fit takes over.

| 2♦ - 2♠ -<br>3♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♠  | 5+♠, 0--2♥
| 3NT | 0--2♥
| 4♣♦ | 5+#, 0--2♥
| 4♥  | 3+♥, 4--7 HCP
| 4NT | Mostly 3+♥, 5+ HCP, slam drive

- **♠K42 ♥972 ♦984 ♣A652:** Bid 4♥.
- **♠KQ4 ♥842 ♦1093 ♣AJ72:** Bid 4NT.

What 4NT asks here was not probed.

### Over 2NT

| 2♦ - 2NT - | |
|-------|-|
| 3♣♦♥♠ | 5+#
| 3NT   | 23--25 HCP, BAL
| 4♥♠   | 7+#, usually 19--21 HCP (probed in ♠ only)
| 4NT   | QUANT, 25--28 HCP, BAL

Responder's 2NT promises no ace, so opener's 4NT is a quantitative raise
rather than an ace ask.  The 2011 writeup does not list it.

| 2♦ - 2NT -<br>3NT - | |
|-------|-|
| Pass  | 8--9 HCP, BAL
| 4♣♦♥♠ | 5+#
| 4NT   | QUANT, 9--12 HCP, BAL

- **♠AQ62 ♥AK63 ♦AK ♣AJ2:** Bid 4NT over 2NT.
- **♠8732 ♥96 ♦K85 ♣KQJ7:** Pass 3NT.
- **♠J104 ♥QJ ♦KQ65 ♣J982:** Bid 4NT over 3NT.

### Over a suit

With 2+ cards in responder's suit, opener raises.  A raise has priority over a
5-card major of opener's own.

| 2♦ - 3x - | |
|----------|-|
| New suit | 5+#, usually 0--1 card in x
| 3NT      | 0--1 card in x
| Raise    | 2+ cards in x

The 2011 writeup has 3NT deny a 5-card major or 6-card minor.  Probing Wbridge5
5.12 shows that a balanced opener raises instead, and 3NT shows a singleton
in responder's suit, as with ♠KQ5 ♥K ♦AK92 ♣AKJ62 over 3♥.

The raise of a minor is 4♣ or 4♦.  After a major, there are two raises.

- **4♥♠:** 2+ cards, usually balanced, 23--26 HCP
- **4NT:** 2+ cards, stronger or more shapely, a slam drive whose answers were
  not probed

- **♠AKQ6 ♥A95 ♦AKQJ43 ♣-:** Bid 3♦ over 3♣.
- **♠AJ4 ♥AK973 ♦AK3 ♣KQ:** Bid 4♣ over 3♣.  Raise rather than show hearts.
- **♠AKJ10 ♥AQJ ♦AK9 ♣K63:** Bid 4♥ over 3♥.
- **♠AKQJ106 ♥AJ6 ♦A ♣AKJ:** Bid 4NT over 3♥.

### Over 3NT

Responder holds two aces, so opener holds at most two.

| 2♦ - 3NT - | |
|-------|-|
| 4♣♦♥♠ | 5+#
| 4NT!  | BAL, asking for kings

| 2♦ - 3NT -<br>4NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 5♣! | No king
| 5♦! | One king
| 5♥! | Two kings

- **♠AKQ10 ♥KQJ5 ♦AJ ♣KQ9:** Bid 4NT over 3NT.
- **♠A6 ♥A103 ♦J8752 ♣J75:** Bid 5♣ over 4NT.
- **♠965 ♥AK1095 ♦K84 ♣A9:** Bid 5♥ over 4NT.

The 2011 writeup also lists 5x for a 7-card suit.  The probes held no such
hand.

### Over 4♣

Responder holds three aces, so opener holds at most one.  Such a hand is too
rare to probe, and the 2011 writeup's rebids were not verified: new suit for
5+ cards, 4NT for a balanced hand, and a jump for 7+ cards.

## Competitive bidding

Over a double, the responses stay the same.  A pass shows a hand that would
bid 2NT, usually with diamond honors to defend 2♦ doubled.

| 2♦ (X) | |
|------|-|
| Pass | No ace, (two kings or 9+ HCP), diamond values
| 2♥!  | No ace, at most one king, 0--8 HCP
| 2♠!  | One ace
| 2NT! | No ace, (two kings or 9+ HCP)
| 3♣♦  | No ace, 6+#
| 3NT! | Two aces
| 4♣!  | Three aces

- **♠J104 ♥QJ ♦KQ65 ♣J982:** Pass.
- **♠K7654 ♥KJ6 ♦8642 ♣10:** Bid 2NT.

Over an overcall of 2♠, only 3NT and 4♣ still show aces.

| 2♦ (2♠) | |
|------|-|
| Pass | 0--8 HCP, 0--1 ace
| X    | 8+ HCP, 0--1 ace, no ♠ stopper
| 2NT  | 8+ HCP, 0--1 ace, usually a ♠ stopper
| 3♣♦♥ | No ace, 6+#
| 3NT! | Two aces
| 4♣!  | Three aces

- **♠8732 ♥96 ♦K85 ♣KQJ7:** Double.
- **♠KQ4 ♥842 ♦1093 ♣AJ72:** Bid 2NT.

Other overcalls were not probed.

## Not probed

- Opener's rebids after 4♣
- What 4NT asks, by responder in 2♦ - 2♠ - 3♥ - 4NT or by opener in
  2♦ - 3♥♠ - 4NT
- 2♦ in the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th seats
- Vulnerability
