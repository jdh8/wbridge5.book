# Responses to preemptive openings

Responder to a [preemptive opening](Preemptive.md) seldom has a game, so most
calls continue the preemption.  The ranges below come from probing Wbridge5
5.12 with partner's opening in 1st seat and nobody vulnerable, unless stated
otherwise.  Raises in this chapter are counted in support points (HCP plus
shortness, see [hand evaluation](../Evaluation.md)).

## Weak 2♥♠

Raises follow the total number of trumps more than strength.  Responder passes
with 3-card support and a weak hand, raises to 3 with 4 cards, and bids game
with 5.  The 3-level raise is not invitational: it covers 2--14 HCP.  With a
strong hand, responder asks with 2NT instead.

| 2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♠   | 11+ HCP, 5+♠, 0--2♥
| 2NT! | Feature ask, 14+ with 3+♥, rarely 2♥
| 3♣♦  | 13--17 HCP, 5+#, 0--2♥
| 3♥   | PRE, up to 14, 4+♥, sometimes 3♥
| 3NT  | S/O, 16--17 HCP, BAL, 2♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 12+ HCP, **0--1#**, 5+♥
| 4♥   | S/O, any strength, 5+♥ or a strong hand without slam interest

| 2♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 2NT! | Feature ask, 14+ with 3+♠, rarely 2♠
| 3♣♦  | 14--16 HCP, 5+#, 0--2♠
| 3♥   | 12+ HCP, 5+♥, 0--2♠
| 3♠   | PRE, up to 14, 4+♠, sometimes 3♠
| 3NT  | S/O, 16+ HCP, 2♠
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 11+ HCP, **0--1#**, 4+♠
| 4♠   | S/O, any strength, 5+♠ or a strong hand without slam interest

Without a fit, responder passes up to about 15 HCP.  For example, responder
passes 2♥ with ♠K ♥K95 ♦AQJ95 ♣10865, 13 HCP.  Vulnerability makes no visible
difference to the raises: at favorable vulnerability, 2♥ - 3♥ was bid on 107
of 300 boards, against 100 with nobody vulnerable.  Wbridge5 bid 3NT only with
16+ HCP and a doubleton in opener's major.

### Seat

Facing a 2nd-seat weak 2♥, responder is more active.  With 12--15 HCP and
3-card support, it bids 2NT or 3♥ and never passes, where facing a 1st-seat
weak 2♥ it passed half of such hands.  It also bids 2NT with a doubleton more
often.  New suits still need about 12 HCP.

A passed hand never bid a new suit with 8--10 HCP and five spades over a
3rd-seat 2♥: it passed on all 265 boards.

### Feature ask 2NT

Opener shows a minimum by rebidding the suit.  With a maximum, opener shows
shortness first, then an ace or king in a side suit, and bids 3NT with
neither.  The help file says only a void is shown, but Wbridge5 also shows a
singleton.

| 2♥ - 2NT - | |
|------|-|
| 3♣♦! | MAX, A or K in the suit
| 3♥   | MIN, 9--11
| 3♠!  | MAX, **0--1♠**
| 3NT  | MAX, no singleton or void, no A or K in a minor
| 4♣♦! | MAX, **0--1#**

The cheap 3♠ shows shortness, so a spade feature is not shown.  Opener with
only a spade feature bids 3NT.

| 2♠ - 2NT - | |
|-------|-|
| 3♣♦♥! | MAX, A or K in the suit
| 3♠    | MIN, 9--11
| 3NT   | MAX, no singleton or void, no side A or K
| 4♣♦♥! | MAX, **0--1#**

The minimum ranges 6--8 HCP over 2♥ and 6--9 HCP over 2♠.  A maximum is
11--14 points, so a singleton lifts some 6--7 HCP hands.  Examples from
2♥ - 2NT:

- **♠Q83 ♥KQ8763 ♦63 ♣64:** Bid 3♥, minimum.
- **♠- ♥AQ10864 ♦1092 ♣9732:** Bid 3♠, a void with only 6 HCP.
- **♠J10 ♥K87653 ♦A52 ♣105:** Bid 3♦, the ace.
- **♠92 ♥AQ10864 ♦Q64 ♣108:** Bid 3NT, nothing to show.

Responder's continuations after the answer were not probed.

### Interference

Over a takeout double or a 2♠ overcall, the raises, 2NT, and splinters keep
their meanings, [systems on](../Glossary.md#systems-on).  Opener's answers to
2NT under interference were not probed.

| 2♥ (X) | |
|------|-|
| XX   | 14+ HCP, 3♥, rarely 2♥
| 2NT! | Feature ask, 13+, 3+♥, usually 4+♥
| 3♥   | Up to 14 HCP, 3+♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 12+ HCP, **0--1#**, 5+♥
| 4♥   | S/O, 5+♥

Without support, responder passes even with 16 HCP.  The redouble does not
look for a penalty.

| 2♥ (2♠) | |
|------|-|
| X    | PEN, 10+ HCP, 3+♠, usually 5+♠
| 2NT! | Feature ask, 14+ with 3+♥
| 3♥   | Up to 14 HCP, 3+♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 12+ HCP, **0--1#**, 5+♥
| 4♥   | S/O, 5+♥

Over a 3-level overcall, the double is still for penalty, and 4 of the
opponents' suit is a strong raise.  Responder needs 3 cards to compete in
opener's suit.

| 2♠ (3♣) | |
|------|-|
| X    | PEN, 3+♣, usually 5+♣
| 3♠   | Up to 14 HCP, 3+♠
| 4♣!  | Cuebid, 11+ HCP, 3+♠, seen on only 6 boards
| 4♠   | 12+ HCP with 3+♠, or 5+♠ with any strength
| 4NT! | [RKCB](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314)

A doubleton is not enough to raise over an overcall.  Wbridge5 raised with a
doubleton on only 2 of 600 boards.

## Preemptive 3s

Responder raises to game with a major fit and 10+ HCP.  A raise to 4 of a
minor continues the preemption.  New suits are natural and only
seen over minors.

| 3♣♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 3♦   | Over 3♣ only, 15 HCP, 5+♦, rare
| 3♥♠  | 11+ HCP, 5+#
| 3NT  | S/O, 13+ HCP
| 4♣♦  | PRE, 0--14 HCP, 4+ cards, or 3 cards with 10+ HCP
| 4NT! | [RKCB](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314), usually 17+ HCP
| 5♣♦  | 5+ cards with a weak hand, or 3+ cards with 11--17 HCP

| 3♥♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 4♥♠  | 10+ HCP and 2+ cards, or 4+ cards with any strength
| 4NT! | [RKCB](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314), 13+ HCP

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that a 3♣ opener answers 4NT as [RKCB
0314](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314) in clubs, counting the ♣K, on all 226 boards.
For example, ♠Q2 ♥54 ♦86 ♣KQ109873 bids 5♦.

Over 3♥♠, responder passed 10--15 HCP hands without a fit.  It bid 3NT on
none of 600 boards and a new suit on one.  Over 3♠, 4♥ was natural with seven
hearts.  Responses over a 2nd-seat 3♣ were the same as over a 1st-seat one.

### Interference

Over a double, a redouble shows a good hand, not a penalty.  Responder
competes with 4-card support and a weak hand.

| 3♥ (X) | |
|----|-|
| XX | 12+ HCP, or less with 5+♥
| 4♥ | Up to 11 HCP, 3+♥, usually 4+♥

Over an overcall, the double is for penalty.

| 3♣ (3♥) | |
|-----|-|
| X   | PEN, 3+♥, usually 5+♥
| 3♠  | 11+ HCP, 5+♠
| 3NT | S/O, 12+ HCP
| 4♣  | Up to 14 HCP, 3+♣
| 4♥! | Cuebid, 14+ HCP, 5+♣, seen on only 5 boards
| 5♣  | 10+ HCP, 3+♣

## Gambling 3NT

The [gambling 3NT](../Openings.md) shows a 7--8-card minor headed by AKQ.
Probing found no side ace or king in any of 46 openings.

| 3NT - | |
|------|-|
| Pass | To play, usually 12+ HCP with stoppers in side suits
| 4♣   | Pass or correct
| 4♦   | NAT, 6+♦ of responder's own
| 4♥♠  | S/O, 6+#
| 6♣♦  | To play, the minor responder can work out from its own top honors

Responder can often tell opener's minor.  For example, with ♠76 ♥A76 ♦1097
♣AKQ94, responder knows that opener holds diamonds and bids 6♦.

| 3NT - 4♣ - | |
|------|-|
| Pass | With clubs
| 4♦   | With diamonds

Opener corrected on all 58 boards.

## Namyats

Responder does not bid the next step over Namyats.  It signs off in opener's
major or asks with RKCB 4NT, usually with 14+ HCP.

| 4♣! - | |
|------|-|
| 4♥   | S/O
| 4♠   | NAT, 8+ HCP, 5+♠
| 4NT! | [RKCB](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314) for hearts

| 4♦! - | |
|------|-|
| 4♥   | NAT, 7+♥, rare
| 4♠   | S/O
| 4NT! | [RKCB](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314) for spades

## 4♥♠ and 5♣♦

Over 4♥♠, responder passes up to about 18 HCP and bids RKCB 4NT with slam interest.
Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that a 4♥ opener answers it as [RKCB
0314](../Slam.md#rkcb-0314) on all 112 boards, ignoring any void.  For
example, ♠J32 ♥AKQJ1092 ♦753 ♣- bids 5♠.

Over 5♣♦, responder raises to 6 with slam values, usually 14+ HCP, although a
quarter of 14+ HCP hands passed.  The raise needs only a singleton in opener's
suit.

| 5♣♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 6♣♦ | Usually 12+ HCP, 1+ cards
| 7NT | Seen twice, with three aces and long side suits

## 4NT

The [4NT opening](../Openings.md) shows 6-6 in the minors or longer.
Responder bids the longer minor, at the 6-level with a good hand, usually 12+
HCP.  With equal minors, responder chose either.

| 4NT! - | |
|-----|-|
| 5♣♦ | Longer minor
| 6♣♦ | Longer minor, 12+ HCP

## Not probed

- Rebids after a new suit by responder, and whether those new suits are
  forcing
- Responses to 3rd- and 4th-seat preempts other than those above, and other
  vulnerabilities
- Interference over 3NT and the 4- and 5-level openings
- Hands under 8 HCP after interference, so the lower ends of those ranges
  are open
- RKCB after Namyats, and after 3♦♥♠ and 4♠ openings (only 3♣ and 4♥ were
  probed)
