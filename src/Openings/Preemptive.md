# Preemptive openings

Preemptive bids are bids higher than usual to aggressively reach a contract,
avoiding defending.  A preemptive opening shows **< 12 HCP**, which encourages
reaching a suit contract and discourages reaching a notrump contract or
defending.

Preemptive openings are available to the first three seats only, where there
is still an opponent to block.  The 3rd seat can make the most aggressive
openings because the partner has passed.

## Weak 2s

The weak 2s require 6+ cards in general, but 5 cards is accepted for the 3rd
seat unless vulnerability is unfavorable.  If non-vulnerable, the first two
seats can also open a weak 2 with AKxxx or AQJxx in a 5-3-3-2 hand.  The
strength requirements are as follows.

In the first 2 seats, **pass** with 4+ cards in the other major.  This prevents
losing a 4-4 fit if partner has an opening hand.

- **Vulnerable:** 9+
- **Not vulnerable:** 7+

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 shows that the 1st and 2nd seats open alike: 300 hands
with 5--7 hearts drew the same calls in both seats at all four
vulnerabilities, as did 300 hands with 6--8 clubs at none and unfavorable.
Mostly only Wbridge5's own vulnerability counts.  On 300 random hands with
4--11 HCP, favorable vulnerability opened exactly as none vulnerable, and both
vulnerable exactly as unfavorable.  With 5--7 hearts and 3--11 HCP, though, 5
of 300 hands opened a step higher at favorable than at none, and 7 of 300 at
both vulnerable than at unfavorable.  For example, ♠J9 ♥Q98653 ♦K76 ♣72
passes at none but opens 2♥ at favorable.  With 6 hearts and 3--10 HCP,
Wbridge5 opened 2♥ on 51 of 75 hands from 4 HCP when not vulnerable, but on
32 from 6 HCP when vulnerable.  The 5-card weak 2s, e.g. ♠54 ♥KQJ97 ♦Q87
♣1042 and ♠92 ♥AK872 ♦843 ♣976, all became passes when vulnerable.  Spades
behave the same: all nine 5-card 2♠ openings among 300 hands with 5--7 spades
became passes when vulnerable, and the 6-card 2♠ fell from 41 of 58 hands to
31.

### Weak 2s in the 3rd seat

In the 3rd seat both sides' vulnerability counts.  With 5--7 hearts and 3--11
HCP, 300 boards per vulnerability:

| Vulnerability | 5♥, 3--9 HCP | 6♥, 3--10 HCP |
|---|---|---|
| Unfavorable | Never 2♥ | 2♥ from 6 HCP, 33 of 75 pass
| Both | 2♥ on 24 of 141, from 6 HCP | 2♥ from 6 HCP, 29 of 75 pass
| None | 2♥ on 35 of 141, from 4 HCP | 2♥ from 4 HCP, 12 of 75 pass
| Favorable | 2♥ on 139 of 141, from 3 HCP | Never passes, 3♥ on 23 from 3 HCP

At unfavorable vulnerability, the 3rd seat also passes most of the light
1-level openings: with 5 hearts and 10--11 HCP, it opened 1♥ on 20 of 62
hands and passed the rest, where none vulnerable opened 1♥ or 2♥ on all but
4.  At favorable vulnerability, those hands opened 2♥ on 38 boards and 1♥ on
23.

## Preemptive 3s

Opening 3 of a suit promises 6+ cards with further requirements.

### First 2 seats

If there are only 6 cards in the suit, it shall be no weaker than KJ10xxx if
minor or KQ10xxx if major.  The required strength is 2 more points than weak 2.

- **Vulnerable:** 11+, 8+ HCP
- **Not vulnerable:** 9+

As with weak 2s, favorable vulnerability opens as none vulnerable in the 1st
seat.  With 6 clubs and 3--10 HCP, Wbridge5 opened 3♣ on 46 of 200 hands
(7--10 HCP) when not vulnerable and on 17 (8--10 HCP) when vulnerable.  With
7--8 clubs it opened 3♣ on 49 of 62 hands from 5 HCP, and on 37 when
vulnerable.  A 7-card major drops a level when vulnerable: 4♥ needed 10+ HCP
instead of 7+, and 5--6 HCP opened 2♥ instead of 3♥.  Diamonds behave as
clubs: with 6 diamonds and 3--10 HCP, 3♦ came on 50 of 193 hands (7--10 HCP)
when not vulnerable and on 17 (8--10 HCP) when vulnerable.  With 7 diamonds it
came on 47 of 58 hands from 5 HCP, and on 36 from 6 HCP when vulnerable.

### The 3rd seat

Quality of a 6-card minor can be as weak as KJxxxx in the 3rd seat, but no
weaker for a major because a rubbish major opens a weak 2 instead.  There are
no more requirements on defensive values because the partnership will seldom
have enough values for 3NT.

- **Unfavorable:** 9+
- **Both vulnerable:** 8+
- **None vulnerable:** 7+
- **Favorable:** 6+.  A 6-card rubbish minor is acceptable here.

Probing Wbridge5 5.12 confirms the order.  With 6 clubs and 3--10 HCP, the
3rd seat opened 3♣ on 154 of 206 hands at favorable vulnerability (from 4
HCP), 86 at none vulnerable (from 4 HCP), 66 at both vulnerable (from 5 HCP)
and 17 at unfavorable (from 8 HCP).
With 7 clubs, it opened 3♣ or 4♣ on 54 of 60 hands at favorable, 46 at none,
39 at both vulnerable and 31 at unfavorable.  The 4♣ opening is natural here, with 7 clubs and 6--9
HCP, e.g. ♠Q2 ♥54 ♦86 ♣KQ109873 at favorable vulnerability.

## Preemptive 4♥♠ {#preemptive-4M}

Opening 4♥♠ promises 7+ cards.  If there are only 7 cards in the suit, it shall
be no weaker than KQxxxxx.

- **Unfavorable:** 14+
- **Both vulnerable:** 13+
- **None vulnerable:** 12+
- **Favorable:** 11+

## Preemptive 5♣♦

Opening 5♣♦ promises 8+ cards.

## Namyats

Namyats uses 4♣ and 4♦ for strong 4♥ and 4♠ openers respectively.  Besides
criteria from 4♥♠ openings, there are further requirements for a Namyats
opening.

- **Suit quality:** KQ10 or stronger if there are only 7 cards
- **Strength:** At most 5 losers
